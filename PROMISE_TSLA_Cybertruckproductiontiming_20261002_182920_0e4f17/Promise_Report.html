[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: Cybertruck production timing

VERDICT: Delayed (confidence: medium)

Management initially pointed to late 2021 / next year for Cybertruck ramp and volume (Q2 2021, Q1 2022, Q2 2022). 
Through 2022-2023 they continued to discuss production readiness, pilot line alpha/release candidates, and early ramp 
challenges, indicating volume volume targets were not reached on the originally implied timeline (Q1 2023, Q2 2023, Q3 
2023). By 2024-2025 they describe ongoing ramp and output/cell production continuing, consistent with a 
later-than-expected start and a phased approach rather than immediate late-2021/early-2022 volume (Q2 2024, Q1 2025).
