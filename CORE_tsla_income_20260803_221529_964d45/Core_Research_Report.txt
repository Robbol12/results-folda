[CORE RESEARCH] TSLA: income

Quarters searched: 8
Filing sections searched: 174

Over the periods from Q1 2021 through Q4 2022, Tesla's net income attributable to common stockholders showed a consistent and substantial increase year-over-year, with net income rising from $438 million in Q1 2021 to $12.56 billion for the full year 2022. The company experienced favorable changes in other income (expense), primarily due to foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations and mark-to-market remeasurements of interest rate swaps, which contributed positively to net income. Management highlighted record production, deliveries, and milestone profitability achievements, such as surpassing $1 billion in GAAP net income in Q2 2021 and accumulating $5.5 billion in net income for 2021. Despite these positive trends, no forward guidance on income was provided by management in any period, limiting the ability to assess forecasting accuracy. The reported figures are consistent across filings and transcripts, with no noted accounting changes affecting income reporting.

Forecasting credibility: None
There were zero guidance/actual pairs found for income, indicating that management did not provide forward guidance on this topic. Therefore, it is not possible to assess forecasting credibility based on guidance versus actual results. Any commentary on income growth or profitability milestones is descriptive and retrospective rather than predictive.

Trends:
  - Consistent year-over-year net income growth
  - Favorable impact from foreign currency exchange rates
  - Positive mark-to-market adjustments on interest rate swaps
  - Increasing scale of profitability milestones

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations affecting other income
  - Mark-to-market remeasurement impacts on income
  - Non-cash expenses and gains influencing net income changes

Key concerns:
  - No forward guidance on income limits ability to evaluate management's forecasting accuracy
  - Variability in other income (expense), net, due to market factors introduces some volatility in net income
  - No accounting or reporting inconsistencies noted across filings

Conclusion:
Tesla's income has shown strong and consistent growth over the analyzed periods, driven by operational improvements and favorable financial factors such as currency exchange and interest rate swap adjustments. However, management has not provided forward guidance on income, precluding an assessment of forecasting credibility. The reported income figures are reliable and consistent across filings, reflecting genuine profitability improvements. Investors should note that income is influenced by both operational performance and market-driven other income components, which can introduce variability. Overall, Tesla's income trajectory is positive, but forecasting transparency on this metric is absent.
