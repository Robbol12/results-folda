[CORE RESEARCH] AMZN: revenue

Quarters searched: 8
Filing sections searched: 130

Over the reported periods from Q1 2021 through Q4 2022, revenue showed consistent growth with management frequently highlighting strong year-over-year increases and accelerating growth rates, particularly in AWS and advertising segments. Early in 2021, revenue notably exceeded guidance ranges, with total net sales reaching $108.5 billion in Q1 2021, surpassing the $100-$106 billion guidance. Throughout 2021, revenue growth remained robust, with AWS revenue growth accelerating to 40% by Q4 2021. In 2022, despite some foreign exchange headwinds, revenue continued to exceed guidance, with Q2 2022 net sales of $121.2 billion surpassing the top end of guidance. Management's guidance became more cautious by late 2022, reflecting expectations of flattish or down growth in some segments, but actual results still met or exceeded expectations overall.

Forecasting credibility: High
Management consistently provided revenue guidance that was met or exceeded across five guidance/actual pairs, demonstrating strong forecasting credibility. The actual revenue figures from filings often surpassed the guidance ranges, and management's commentary aligned well with reported results. The presence of precise filing-sourced actuals further supports the reliability of the guidance and results comparison.

Trends:
  - Consistent year-over-year revenue growth
  - Acceleration in AWS revenue growth
  - Increasing contribution from advertising revenue
  - Foreign exchange headwinds impacting growth rates
  - Cautious guidance in late 2022

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Impact of foreign exchange rates on revenue
  - Sustaining high growth rates post-pandemic
  - Reliance on AWS and advertising for growth
  - Unearned revenue recognition timing

Key concerns:
  - No significant accounting changes or inconsistencies were noted across filings; revenue recognition policies remained consistent with gross recording of product sales and net recording of service sales.
  - Foreign exchange impacts introduced variability in reported growth rates, requiring careful interpretation of year-over-year comparisons.

Conclusion:
Revenue demonstrated strong and consistent growth throughout the periods analyzed, with management frequently exceeding their guidance ranges. The company achieved significant increases in total net sales, driven by both product and service segments, notably AWS and advertising. Despite some foreign exchange challenges, actual revenue results consistently met or surpassed expectations, underscoring management's effective forecasting and operational execution. The revenue run rate increased substantially, reaching over $85 billion annualized by Q4 2022, reflecting sustained business expansion.
