Over the reported periods in 2022, revenue growth showed a consistent upward trend with management highlighting strong constant currency revenue growth and improving underlying business trends. Actual reported figures from filings confirm steady increases in net sales and comparable sales across key segments such as Walmart U.S. and Sam's Club, with growth rates generally rising quarter over quarter. Inflation and increased average ticket sizes contributed to revenue growth, while operating expenses as a percentage of sales increased modestly due to wage costs and supply chain factors. Management also noted significant growth in digital sales and operating income over the fiscal year. However, no explicit forward guidance on revenue growth was provided by management during these periods.

Conclusion:
Revenue growth in 2022 was robust and consistent, supported by strong comparable sales increases and higher average ticket sizes across major segments. Inflation and pricing strategies contributed positively to revenue, while digital sales gains also supported overall growth. Despite rising operating expenses, the company expanded its revenue base significantly year-over-year. However, the absence of explicit forward guidance on revenue growth means that management's ability to forecast this metric cannot be judged from the available data.
