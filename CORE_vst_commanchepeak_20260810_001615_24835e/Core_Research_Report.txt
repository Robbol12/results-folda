Over the reported periods from Q1 2021 through Q4 2022, the disclosures related to Comanche Peak primarily focus on the nuclear decommissioning trust investments, the plant's operational capacity, and risk management actions. The fair market value of the decommissioning trust investments showed a gradual increase over 2021. The company consistently disclosed that if the trust funds are inadequate, Oncor would be responsible for collecting additional amounts from customers, with no obligation on Vistra, provided compliance with regulations. In late 2022, Vistra submitted an application for license renewal of the Comanche Peak plant, indicating a forward-looking operational intent. Insurance coverage details and assumptions about license extensions were also disclosed, reflecting ongoing risk management and regulatory considerations.
