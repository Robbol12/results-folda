Over the 2015 reporting periods, the company demonstrated consistent organic sales growth driven primarily by increased sales volume rather than price increases. Growth was supported both by existing customer programs and incremental sales from acquisitions, with organic growth accounting for a significant portion of total sales increases each quarter. Accounts receivable and working capital increases aligned with the reported sales growth, indicating expanding customer activity and demand.
