Longitudinal Summary

Over the course of 2024, management consistently projected modest revenue growth or flat organic revenue, often citing low single-digit increases or flat performance for the full year. However, actual results repeatedly fell short of these expectations, with several quarters showing slight declines in total and organic revenue. Despite some positive regional performance, such as in Asia Pacific and Europe, overall revenue trends were challenged by weaker demand in key segments like Construction Products. The company also experienced impacts from divestitures and accounting changes, such as the LIFO method adjustment, which affected cost of revenue but not the top-line figures directly. By year-end, revenues were down in the 3% to 5% range, missing the earlier guidance of flat to low single-digit growth.

Forecasting Credibility: Low

Management issued six guidance/actual pairs related to revenue, with the majority being missed. Despite repeated guidance of flat or slightly positive organic revenue growth, actual results showed declines or flat performance below expectations. This consistent pattern of missing revenue targets undermines the credibility of management's forecasting. The presence of precise filing data confirming these misses further supports a low rating for forecasting reliability.

Longitudinal Summary

Over the course of 2024, the company consistently reported improving operating margins across multiple segments, with several quarters setting new records. Management emphasized the role of enterprise initiatives as a key driver of margin expansion, contributing between 100 and 140 basis points each quarter. Despite some organic revenue declines and moderating demand, operating margins improved steadily, with Q3 and Q4 showing particularly strong results that exceeded guidance. The company maintained a clear long-term target of achieving a 30% operating margin by 2030, with intermediate goals of low to mid-20% margins by 2026 and the next few years. Guidance was generally conservative and was either met or exceeded in all reported periods, reinforcing management's focus on quality growth and operational leverage.

The actual operating margins reported in filings consistently matched or surpassed the guided ranges, demonstrating reliable forecasting. The use of precise filing-sourced actuals further supports the credibility of management's forecasts.
