[CORE RESEARCH] TSLA: losses

Quarters searched: 8
Filing sections searched: 174

Over the reported periods from Q1 2021 through Q4 2022, the company consistently recorded impairment losses primarily related to the carrying value of bitcoin holdings. These impairment losses increased from $27 million in Q1 2021 to $204 million for the full year 2022, reflecting volatility in digital asset valuations. The filings also note gains on sales or conversions of bitcoin, which partially offset these impairments. The allowance for credit losses remained relatively stable, with minor fluctuations noted between $41 million and $45 million. Management disclosed that impairment losses are recognized in the period identified and that foreign exchange gains and losses vary with currency movements, but no forward guidance on losses was provided.

Forecasting credibility: None
There are zero guidance versus actual pairs available for losses, as management does not appear to provide forward guidance on impairment losses or other loss-related items. Consequently, it is not possible to assess forecasting credibility for this topic based on the evidence. The assessment is strictly limited to reported actuals without any forecast comparison.

Trends:
  - Increasing impairment losses over time
  - Stable allowance for credit losses
  - Consistent recognition of impairment losses in period identified
  - Gains on bitcoin sales partially offset impairments
  - Foreign exchange gains/losses vary with currency movements

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Volatility in bitcoin valuation impacting impairment losses
  - Foreign exchange rate fluctuations affecting gains and losses
  - Potential losses from products liability and other claims
  - Non-cash impairment losses recognized in restructuring and other

Key concerns:
  - No forward guidance on losses limits ability to evaluate management's forecasting accuracy for this topic
  - Impairment losses are significant and increasing, which may impact earnings volatility
  - Allowance for credit losses shows minor fluctuations but no clear trend or explanation

Conclusion:
Losses, particularly impairment losses on bitcoin holdings, have been a material and increasing component of the company's financial results over the periods reviewed. These losses are recognized promptly when identified, reflecting the volatility of digital asset valuations. While gains on bitcoin sales provide some offset, the net effect is a growing impairment charge. The absence of forward guidance on losses means forecasting credibility cannot be assessed, and investors should be aware of the inherent unpredictability and potential earnings impact from these impairments.
