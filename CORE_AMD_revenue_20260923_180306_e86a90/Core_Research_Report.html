Longitudinal Summary

Across FY2024, management’s revenue narrative shifted from early-year growth expectations to progressively lower or inconsistent guidance ranges as data center and client strength did not fully offset weakness in gaming and embedded. In Q2 2024, management guided revenue to about $6.7B (±$300M) while the quarter ended at $5.8B, a miss versus the midpoint. In Q3 2024, multiple revenue-related guides (including “approximately $7.5B ± $300M” for Q4 and earlier FY2024 revenue commentary) were again missed, with Q3 revenue at $6.8B and subsequent Q4 revenue at $7.7B. For Q4 specifically, the company guided $7.1B (±$300M) and the quarter closed at $7.7B, which falls within the stated range but above the midpoint, while embedded and gaming remained weaker than implied by overall optimism. Overall, the filing-sourced results confirm strong annual revenue growth but with quarterly volatility that repeatedly diverged from management’s numerical revenue guidance.
