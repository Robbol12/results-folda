Quarters searched: 4

Over the course of 2024, gross margin trends showed a mixed pattern across segments. The total automotive segment experienced a general decline in gross margin percentages year-over-year, dropping from around 21.1% in early 2024 to 18.4% by year-end. Conversely, the energy generation and storage segment saw a significant improvement in gross margin, rising from 11.0% in Q1 to 26.2% by year-end. The total automotive & services and other segment mostly followed the automotive segment's downward trend, with some fluctuations including a brief increase in Q3. Management attributed changes primarily to factors such as vehicle average selling price, Cybertruck ramp, cost per unit, and regulatory credits.

Recurring themes/risks:

Key concerns:
  - No forward-looking guidance on gross margin limits ability to evaluate management's forecasting accuracy.
  - Gross margin declines in automotive segments may reflect pricing pressures or cost challenges despite some cost improvements.
  - Inconsistencies in segment margin trends suggest operational or market risks affecting profitability.

Conclusion:
Gross margin for the total automotive segment declined steadily throughout 2024, ending the year at 18.4%, down from 21.1% in early 2024 and 19.4% the prior year. This decline was attributed to lower average selling prices and the Cybertruck ramp, partially offset by cost reductions and increased regulatory credit and FSD revenue. Meanwhile, the energy generation and storage segment showed strong margin improvement, more than doubling from 11.0% to 26.2% over the year. The total automotive & services and other segment generally mirrored the automotive segment's downward margin trend, with some short-term fluctuations. Overall, the gross margin picture reflects mixed operational dynamics with no forward guidance provided to assess management's forecasting reliability.
