Across 2024, total revenue remained pressured by customer inventory digestion and weaker segments. Q1 2024 total revenue was $12.7 billion (up $1.0 billion or 9% vs. Q1 2023), but the quarter’s strength was not broad-based: Other revenue was $391 million (down $90 million) while NEX was cited as down 8% year over year. In Q2 2024, total revenue was $12.8 billion (down $116 million from Q2 2023) with mixed segment performance (CCG up 9%, DCAI down 3%, NEX down 1%), indicating continued volatility rather than a sustained recovery. By Q3 2024, total revenue rose to $13.3 billion but was still down $874 million vs. Q3 2023, and the filing attributes weakness to Altera revenue down 44% and external Intel Foundry down 79%, partially offset by DCAI growth. For full-year 2024, revenue was $17.5 billion (down $1.4 billion vs. 2023) and Altera revenue was $1.5 billion (down $1.3 billion), while Other revenue fell to $3.8 billion (down $1.8 billion), reinforcing that the year ended materially lower versus 2023.

Conclusion:
Revenue in 2024 ended down versus 2023, with full-year operating segment revenue of $17.5 billion (down $1.4 billion from 2023) and additional declines in key lines. The year’s pattern started with Q1 total revenue of $12.7 billion (up $1.0 billion or 9% y/y), but by Q2 and especially Q3, declines reasserted, including total revenue down $116 million y/y in Q2 and down $874 million y/y in Q3. The filing attributes major headwinds to Altera weakness (down 44% in Q3; $1.5 billion in 2024 down $1.3 billion vs. 2023) and external Intel Foundry (down 79% in Q3), partially offset at times by DCAI growth. These disclosed drivers suggest the primary risk to revenue was not just market softness but also segment-specific inventory digestion and structural weakness in Altera/external foundry volumes.
