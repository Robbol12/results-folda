THE ANSWER

What the sources show

Across the 2024 quarters shown, Apple’s revenue growth is consistently described as modest, with net sales up 2% year over year in Q1 2024 driven primarily by iPhone and Services, partially offset by declines in iPad and Wearables, Home and Accessories. By Q3 2024, operating expenses are disclosed with net sales percentages, but the excerpt does not provide a specific net sales growth rate, indicating fewer direct revenue-growth details in the provided evidence. In Q4 2024, management explicitly cites products revenue up 4% year over year and reiterates expectations that total company revenue will grow low to mid-single digits, while also guiding services revenue to grow double digits at a similar rate to fiscal 2024. Management also references product and ecosystem drivers (e.g., Apple Intelligence rolling out over time) when responding to questions about growth, without providing incremental detail on iPhone or China. Deferred revenue timing is also updated from Q1 to Q3, but the excerpts do not explicitly link those changes to revenue-growth performance.

Watch points

  • The Q1 excerpt shows specific segment drivers and offsets, but later excerpts provide less direct quantitative revenue growth detail (e.g., Q3 excerpt focuses on operating expenses percentages rather than sales growth).
  • Deferred revenue realization percentages shift between Q1 and Q3 (66% to <1 year in Q1 vs. 64% in Q3, and 26% to 25% in the 1–2 year bucket), but the excerpts do not explain whether this affects revenue growth or reflects accounting/contracting changes.
  • In Q4, management reiterates company-level growth expectations and services direction but declines to provide iPhone-level growth color, including in response to a China-related question.
  • Revenue mix offset: iPhone and Services strength partially offset by weakness in iPad and Wearables, Home and Accessories (Q1 evidence)
  • Dependence on services growth trajectory (double-digit guidance; referenced continuity from fiscal 2024)
  • Limited disclosure on iPhone/Country specifics despite analyst questions (Q4 response with reduced granularity)
