Change detected: True

In Q4 2024, management framed Apple Intelligence rollout as a new iPhone era with a deliberately slower “over time” 
cadence, explicitly tying it to iPhone 16 upgrade motivation and giving a clear expectation of features/languages 
expanding beyond the initial launch. In Q3 2025, management’s Apple Intelligence discussion is more about execution 
progress and platform-wide availability (including release timing “next year”) and less about directly forecasting 
iPhone demand strength from the rollout; instead, iPhone demand-related commentary in this excerpt focuses on 
pull-forward dynamics (tariffs) rather than Apple Intelligence. Overall, emphasis shifts from rollout-as-upgrade-driver 
(Q4 2024) to rollout progress across Siri/features and then broader demand drivers like tariffs/pull-forward (Q3 2025).
