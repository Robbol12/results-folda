The company's disclosure on supply chain issues evolved from initial COVID-19 related impacts and costs in early 2021 to
a broader discussion of ongoing global supply chain constraints and labor market challenges through 2022. Early filings 
highlighted significant COVID-19 related costs and fulfillment network capacity constraints, with sales growth partially
offset by these issues. Over time, the language expanded to include inflation, geopolitical developments, and regional 
labor market constraints as continuing risks. The company consistently disclosed the lack of long-term supplier 
arrangements and risks related to supplier limitations or delays. Quantitatively, fulfillment and cost of sales expenses
increased notably in 2021 and 2022, reflecting these challenges. By late 2022, the disclosures incorporated more 
cautious forward-looking statements emphasizing the unpredictability of supply chain impacts and labor availability.
