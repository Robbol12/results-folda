Quarters searched: 4
Filing sections searched: 82

Across 2021, management repeatedly reported record revenue quarters (Q1: $111.4B; Q2: $89.6B; Q3: $81.4B; Q4: September quarter $83.4B) alongside double-digit year-over-year growth. Guidance on revenue was largely not provided due to COVID-19 uncertainty in the near term, leaving only limited, narrower quantitative guidance such as supply-constrained revenue impact in Q2. Where revenue-related guidance was given (FY2021 “well over $300B” in Q1 2021, and a Q2 supply-constrained impact of $3–$4B), the later realized results were consistent with upside/strong performance, with FY2021 revenue reported as $366B in Q4 2021 filings. By the end of the period, revenue momentum remained strong, supported by record services and resilient product demand despite supply constraints referenced in the call.

This indicates revenue outperformance versus the provided full-year target, with strong products and record services offsetting or outpacing COVID and supply-constraint risks referenced during guidance.
