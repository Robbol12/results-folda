  - Impact of acquisitions and amortization expenses noted

Recurring themes/risks:

  - Lack of quantitative profitability targets or actual results in filings or transcripts restricts assessment of performance.

Profitability remains a significant area of risk for the company, primarily due to uncertainties in newer business activities, inventory management challenges, and the financial impacts of acquisitions. Without quantitative guidance or results, it is not possible to definitively assess the company's profitability trajectory or management's effectiveness in managing profitability.
