[CORE RESEARCH] TSLA: gross margin

Quarters searched: 8
Filing sections searched: 174

Over the periods from Q1 2021 through Q4 2022, gross margin trends showed a general improvement in automotive gross margins, rising from mid-20% levels to peaks above 30% in early 2022 before declining slightly toward the end of 2022. Energy generation and storage gross margins were more volatile, initially negative and improving to positive by the end of 2022. Management frequently discussed gross margin strength and headwinds, particularly in energy storage and Solar Roof segments, and highlighted cost reductions, factory utilization, and product mix as key drivers. Despite some sequential declines in automotive gross margin in mid-2022, the overall trend was upward compared to 2020 and early 2021 levels.

Forecasting credibility: None
No explicit forward guidance paired with actual results on gross margin was provided in the evidence, so it is not possible to assess management's forecasting credibility for this topic. Management commentary often expressed optimism or uncertainty but did not provide quantifiable guidance figures to compare against actuals. Therefore, no credibility rating can be assigned based on guidance-actual pairs.

Trends:
  - Automotive gross margin generally increasing through 2021 into early 2022
  - Energy generation and storage gross margin initially negative, improving by end 2022
  - Peak automotive gross margin above 30% in early 2022
  - Slight automotive gross margin decline in mid to late 2022
  - Improvement in services and other segment gross margin over time

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Impact of product mix on gross margin, especially Solar Roof and energy storage
  - Manufacturing underutilization during product ramps
  - Regulatory credits affecting automotive gross margin
  - Cost reduction efforts and factory utilization as margin drivers
  - Uncertainty in near-term gross margin due to headwinds and tailwinds

Key concerns:
  - No direct forward guidance on gross margin provided, limiting ability to assess forecasting accuracy
  - Energy generation and storage segment showed significant volatility and negative margins for extended periods
  - Some sequential declines in automotive gross margin in 2022 despite prior gains

Conclusion:
Gross margin for the automotive segment showed a strong upward trajectory from 2020 through early 2022, reaching record highs above 30% GAAP and excluding credits, driven by cost reductions, improved factory utilization, and product mix shifts. However, margins softened somewhat in the latter half of 2022, reflecting challenges such as price cuts and operational inefficiencies. The energy generation and storage segment experienced negative gross margins early on due to product ramp challenges and manufacturing underutilization but improved to positive territory by the end of 2022. Overall, the company demonstrated an ability to improve automotive gross margins significantly over time, though volatility remains in the energy segment. Due to the absence of explicit forward guidance on gross margin, management's forecasting credibility on this metric cannot be evaluated.
