[CORE RESEARCH] TSLA: gross profit

Quarters searched: 8
Filing sections searched: 174

Over the reported periods from Q1 2021 through Q4 2022, gross profit and gross margin for the automotive segment showed a consistent upward trend, reaching record highs in early 2022 before experiencing some sequential declines later in 2022. Management frequently highlighted improvements in automotive gross margin excluding regulatory credits, with margins increasing from the low-to-mid 20% range in early 2021 to above 30% in Q1 2022. Energy generation and storage gross margins were more volatile, initially negative but improving significantly by the end of 2022. The company attributed margin improvements to cost reductions, factory utilization, and product mix changes, while noting some temporary underutilization and product phase-outs impacting energy margins.

Forecasting credibility: None
No explicit forward guidance paired with actual results was provided for gross profit or gross margin, preventing any assessment of management's forecasting credibility on this topic. All commentary on gross profit was retrospective or qualitative without quantitative forward targets. Therefore, no credibility rating can be assigned based on guidance-actual comparisons.

Trends:
  - Automotive gross margin steadily increased through 2021
  - Automotive gross margin peaked in early 2022
  - Energy gross margin improved from negative to positive by end 2022
  - Sequential automotive gross margin declines in mid to late 2022

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Impact of regulatory credits on gross margin
  - Cost reduction and factory utilization as margin drivers
  - Energy segment margin volatility due to product mix and ramp
  - Temporary manufacturing underutilization in energy business

Key concerns:
  - No forward guidance on gross profit or margin was provided, limiting transparency on management's expectations.
  - Energy segment gross margin showed significant fluctuations and negative margins early on, indicating operational challenges.
  - Some inconsistencies in margin presentation, e.g., excluding regulatory credits or leases, complicate direct comparisons.

Conclusion:
Gross profit in the automotive segment demonstrated a clear improvement trend from 2021 through early 2022, with gross margins rising from approximately 25-26% to over 30%, driven by cost efficiencies and increased production. However, margins softened somewhat in the latter half of 2022, reflecting operational challenges and possibly product mix shifts. The energy segment's gross profit was initially negative but improved substantially by the end of 2022, turning positive due to higher volumes and cost reductions. Despite these positive trends, the absence of forward guidance on gross profit or margin means management's ability to forecast this metric cannot be evaluated, and investors must rely on reported results and qualitative commentary alone.
