[CORE RESEARCH] MSCI: revenue growth

Quarters searched: 2
Filing sections searched: 39

Over the course of 2023, the company demonstrated consistent revenue growth across multiple reporting periods, with total operating revenues increasing steadily from 5.8% in Q1 to 12.5% for the full year. Recurring subscription revenues showed strong and sustained growth, particularly in Index products, ESG and Climate products, and Analytics products. Asset-based fees experienced some fluctuations, with decreases early in the year but a rebound by year-end. Non-recurring revenues also saw significant increases by Q4, driven by fees related to unlicensed content usage and licensed data products. Overall, the revenue growth trend was positive and broad-based across product lines.

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance versus actual pairs were provided for revenue growth, so it is not possible to assess management's forecasting credibility on this topic. Without forward guidance figures paired with actual results, no pattern of met, exceeded, or missed expectations can be established. Therefore, the credibility rating must remain null.

Trends:
  - steady total revenue growth
  - strong recurring subscription growth
  - growth in Index, ESG, and Analytics products
  - early-year decline then recovery in asset-based fees
  - significant increase in non-recurring revenues by year-end

Recurring themes/risks:
  - dependence on recurring subscription revenue
  - fluctuations in asset-based fees
  - growth driven by ESG and Climate products
  - impact of foreign currency exchange rates

Key concerns:
  - No forward guidance on revenue growth was provided, limiting ability to assess forecasting accuracy.
  - Some inconsistency in reported growth rates for non-recurring revenues between excerpts (64.3% vs. 75.7%) in Q4 filings may indicate reporting or rounding differences.

Conclusion:
The company exhibited consistent and solid revenue growth throughout 2023, driven primarily by recurring subscription revenues in Index, ESG, and Analytics products. While asset-based fees showed some volatility, they improved by year-end. Non-recurring revenues contributed meaningfully to growth in Q4. However, the absence of explicit forward guidance on revenue growth prevents an evaluation of management's forecasting reliability. Overall, the evidence supports a positive revenue growth trajectory for the year.

Over the reported period, management consistently highlighted that the majority of revenue (96%) comes from subscription-based or recurring sources, with a smaller portion (4%) from nonrecurring product sales. The company noted that demand remains nascent in emerging markets, where adoption of risk management and ESG practices is still developing. Management also acknowledged potential risks to demand if clients become more self-sufficient, which could reduce the need for their products and services. However, no forward guidance on demand was provided, and actual demand figures were not explicitly disclosed beyond qualitative commentary.

  - uncertainty in demand development
