[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: Cybertruck production timing

VERDICT: Delayed (confidence: medium)

Management repeatedly targeted volume production timing: Q2 2021 referenced moving into Cybertruck beta later in 2021 and ramping in production; Q1 2022 said they were on track for volume production next year; and Q2 2022 indicated Cybertruck production in the middle of next year. However, by Q1 2023 they were still building alpha versions on a pilot line, and Q3 2023 discussed early phase ramp with challenges to reaching volume production and output not reaching the next-year rate. Later updates (e.g., Q2 2024 and Q1 2025) still describe ongoing ramp activity rather than completed timing delivery, implying delays versus the earlier “next year/middle of next year” expectations.

  - "Regarding the Cybertruck, we continue to build Alpha versions of the Cybertruck on our pilot line for testing purposes."
