Over the periods from Q1 2019 through Q4 2019, management occasionally referenced guidance in terms of delivery targets and growth expectations, such as the 90,000 to 100,000 2Q deliveries and sequential growth percentages. However, these references were not paired with actual results in the provided evidence, and the filings primarily focused on accounting guidance updates and clarifications rather than forward-looking operational guidance. By Q4 2019, management acknowledged that guidance is based on assumptions and may be provided intermittently, but no concrete guidance-actual comparisons were documented. The filings also highlighted the risks and uncertainties that could cause actual results to deviate from guidance.

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance-actual pairs were found in the evidence, so it is not possible to assess management's forecasting credibility for this topic. Without documented comparisons of guidance versus actual results, any rating would be speculative. The commentary and filings focus more on accounting guidance and risk disclosures rather than consistent forward guidance and its accuracy.

Trends:
  - Intermittent delivery guidance references
  - Focus on accounting and regulatory guidance
  - Acknowledgment of assumptions and risks in guidance

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Uncertainty in meeting assumptions underlying guidance
  - Potential impact of risks on financial performance
  - Accounting standards updates affecting disclosures

Key concerns:
  - No documented guidance-actual pairs limit ability to evaluate forecasting accuracy
  - Guidance references are vague and not consistently quantified
  - Accounting guidance updates may complicate comparability across periods

Conclusion:
Management occasionally provides forward-looking guidance, particularly around deliveries and growth, but the evidence lacks any paired actual results to verify the accuracy of these forecasts. The filings emphasize that guidance is based on assumptions and subject to risks that could cause deviations, but without concrete guidance-actual comparisons, the credibility of management's forecasts cannot be judged. Investors should be cautious in relying on guidance alone and consider the disclosed risks and accounting changes that may affect reported results.
