[CORE RESEARCH] TSLA: gross margin

Quarters searched: 8
Filing sections searched: 186

Across 2023, management discussed gross margin largely in the context of automotive pricing actions and one-time items, while reported gross margins for automotive declined sharply: total automotive gross margin fell from 32.9% (Q1 2022) to 21.1% (Q1 2023), and continued to remain below prior-year levels through each quarter (e.g., 27.9% to 19.2% in Q2 2023; 29.6% to 19.7% in nine months ended Q3 2023). In 2023, energy generation and storage gross margin improved materially (e.g., -11.7% to 11.0% in Q1 2023 and 7.4% to 18.9% for the year ended 2023), partially offsetting automotive weakness in consolidated segment reporting. In 2024, the automotive gross margin remained depressed on a year-to-date basis (e.g., total automotive&services and other fell to 16.7% in Q1 2024 vs 19.9% in Q1 2023; 19.0% to 16.9% in six months ended Q2 2024), but showed some quarter-to-quarter stabilization and lift (e.g., automotive&services and other rose from 17.4% to 18.7% in Q3 2024). By full-year 2024, automotive gross margin&services and other decreased to 16.9% (from 18.2% in 2023) while total automotive decreased to 18.4% (from 19.4%), with improvements in cost/unit metrics and FSD/regulatory credit revenue only partially offsetting lower vehicle ASP and Cybertruck ramp effects.

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance/actual pairs specific to gross margin were identified beyond a general statement about targeting mid-20% gross margins for programs launched (mid-20% for any program), but there is no time-matched “guidance vs actual” outcome pairing count provided for evaluation. As a result, forecasting credibility cannot be assessed from a guidance-versus-actual record for this topic. The available commentary also frames auto gross margin prediction as “extremely challenging,” which further limits the interpretability of any implied forecast.

Trends:
  - Automotive gross margin declined materially in 2023 versus prior-year periods (e.g., 32.9% to 21.1% in Q1; 27.9% to 19.2% in Q2).
  - Energy generation and storage gross margin improved strongly throughout 2023 and further in 2024 (e.g., -11.7% to 11.0% in Q1 2023; 18.9% to 26.2% in full-year 2024).
  - 2024 automotive gross margin remained below 2023 levels on a YTD basis, with some sequential quarter recoveries (e.g., automotive&services and other 17.4% to 18.7% in Q3 2024).
  - Full-year 2024: automotive gross margin continued to soften overall despite offsetting benefits from lower costs and higher regulatory/FSD revenue.

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Automotive gross margin impacted by vehicle pricing/affordability actions, changes in average selling price (ASP), and product ramp dynamics (e.g., Cybertruck).
  - One-time items and warranty adjustments affecting automotive gross margin (notably described in connection with 2023 Q1).
  - Regulatory credits and FSD revenue acting as partial offsets to weaker pricing/mix.
  - Management’s view that auto gross margin forecasting is difficult due to moving parts, including tariffs and local incentives.

Key concerns:
  - Management initially pointed to mid-20% gross margins for programs launched, but reported automotive gross margin in 2023 fell well below that range in each quarter cited (e.g., 21.1% in Q1 2023; 19.2% in Q2 2023; 19.7% in nine months ended Q3 2023), suggesting the stated target did not translate into achieved near-term consolidated automotive gross margins.
  - Quarterly/annual automotive gross margin movements appear heavily driven by factors that are difficult to predict (pricing actions, incentives/tariffs, one-time items, and regulatory/FSD revenue), raising uncertainty about forward-looking comparability.
  - Reported “gross margin” is presented both for “total automotive” and for “total automotive & services and other segment,” which can diverge; interpretation requires careful attention to which line is used when assessing the margin level.

Conclusion:
Gross margin evolution shows a clear split between segments: automotive gross margin deteriorated substantially versus prior-year levels throughout 2023 and remained pressured through 2024. Specifically, total automotive gross margin declined from 32.9% to 21.1% in the three months ended March 31, 2023 and similarly fell to 18.5% in the three months ended March 31, 2024; for the year ended December 31, 2024, total automotive gross margin decreased to 18.4% from 19.4% in 2023. By contrast, energy generation and storage gross margin improved from -11.7% to 11.0% in Q1 2023 and increased to 26.2% for the year ended 2024. Overall gross margin dynamics were therefore dominated by automotive weakness partially offset by improving energy storage margins and some automotive offsets from regulatory credits/FSD revenue, with 2024 automotive margin ending lower than 2023 despite cost and revenue-support factors.
