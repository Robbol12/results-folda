Across the provided periods, Apple’s “revenue cold presentation benchmark” is only evidenced via a limited set of actual revenue statements in transcripts (e.g., Q1 2020 and Q4 2020) and related SEC disclosures focused on presentation/reclassification (e.g., ASU 2017-12 and reclassifications of prior-period amounts). In Q1 2020, management explicitly states revenue was above the high end of a guidance range, indicating a benchmark relative to communicated targets, but the forward guidance specifics for this topic are not paired to an exact subsequent filing actual for an explicit guidance-versus-actual comparison beyond that single statement. Q4 2020 emphasizes record revenue performance and provides a specific iPad revenue figure, but no explicit guidance range or target is provided in the excerpts to benchmark against. For later periods (e.g., Q1 2021), the filing excerpts shown are predominantly about accounting/presentation updates and reclassifications rather than revenue benchmark comparisons.
