[PROMISE TRACKING] NVDA: Blackwell production ramp

VERDICT: Fulfilled (confidence: medium)

Management initially framed Blackwell availability and a production ramp start in Q4 2025 (Q1/Q2 2025), with Q2 stating ramp was scheduled to begin in Q4 and continue into FY26. By Q3 2025, they said the ramp was already in “full steam,” and by Q4 2025 they reported that Blackwell ramp had commenced and production was “in full-year across multiple configurations,” alongside “incredibly ramped up” Grace Blackwell and delivered ~$11B of Blackwell revenue. While earlier quarters used more tentative language, the Q4 evidence indicates delivery at scale around the promised timeframe.
