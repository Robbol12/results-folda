[PROMISE TRACKING] NVDA: Blackwell production ramp

VERDICT: Fulfilled (confidence: medium)

Management initially framed Blackwell availability and a production ramp start in Q4 2025 (Q1/Q2 2025), with Q2 stating 
ramp was scheduled to begin in Q4 and continue into FY26. By Q3 2025, they said the ramp was already in “full steam,” 
and by Q4 2025 they reported that Blackwell ramp had commenced and production was “in full-year across multiple 
configurations,” alongside “incredibly ramped up” Grace Blackwell and delivered ~$11B of Blackwell revenue. While 
earlier quarters used more tentative language, the Q4 evidence indicates delivery at scale around the promised 
timeframe.

Key quotes:
  - “Blackwell production ramp is scheduled to begin in the fourth quarter and continue into fiscal year '26.” 
(NVDA_Q2_2025)
  - “Blackwell production is in full steam.” (NVDA_Q3_2025)
  - And we successfully and incredibly ramped up Grace Blackwell, delivering some $11 billion of revenues last quarter.
