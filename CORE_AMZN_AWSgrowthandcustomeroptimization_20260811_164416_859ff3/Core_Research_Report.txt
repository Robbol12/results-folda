[CORE RESEARCH] AMZN: AWS growth

Quarters searched: 4
Filing sections searched: 66

Throughout the period, AWS demonstrated consistent growth in net sales, with year-over-year increases ranging from 27% to 37%. Management's guidance on infrastructure spend supporting AWS growth was consistently met or exceeded. However, in Q4 2022, management expressed uncertainty about forecasting AWS growth beyond the quarter due to optimization efforts potentially hindering growth in the near term.

Forecasting credibility: High
Management consistently provided guidance on infrastructure spend supporting AWS growth, which was subsequently met or exceeded in each period.

Trends:
  - Consistent growth in AWS net sales
  - Management focus on investing in technology infrastructure to support AWS growth

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Strong customer engagement and innovation driving AWS growth
  - Increased investments in technology infrastructure to support AWS business growth

Key concerns:
  - Management's uncertainty about forecasting AWS growth beyond Q4 2022 could raise questions about future guidance reliability

Conclusion:
AWS demonstrated strong and consistent growth in net sales throughout the period, with management effectively guiding on the infrastructure spend supporting this growth. The consistent execution on these guidance points enhances management's credibility in forecasting AWS growth. However, the expressed uncertainty about forecasting AWS growth beyond Q4 2022 raises a potential red flag for future guidance reliability, which investors should monitor closely.

Evidence Coverage: 99.1% complete; 213/215 claims complete. This is an evidence inventory, not an objective truth score.

=== Q3 2022 ===
Management said:
  - I would say that although we had a 28% growth rate for the quarter for AWS, the back end of the quarter, we were more in the mid-20% growth rate.
  - And this is offset by an approximately $10 billion year-over-year increase in technology infrastructure, primarily to support the rapid growth, innovation and continued expansion of our AWS footprint.
  - In AWS, net sales increased to $20.5 billion in Q3, up 28% year-over-year, excluding the impact of foreign exchange, and now representing an annualized sales run rate of $82 billion.
  - We probably cut about one-third of our budget from what we originally thought for 2022 while still focusing our capital dollars really on the AWS business and increasing customer demand or capacity for increasing customer demand in our stores business.
  - We continue to ramp up our investments in AWS, adding product builders and sales and professional services headcount to help customers save money, invent more quickly in their businesses and transition to the cloud.
  - The broad disclaimer on AWS margins is that they will fluctuate over time as we balance investments versus renegotiating pricing with the long-term customer commitments, all as headwinds to the business, offset by increasing productivity and efficiencies in our data centers, which drive profitability.
