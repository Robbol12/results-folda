[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: Cybertruck production timing

VERDICT: Delayed (confidence: medium)

Management initially targeted ramp/volume production for Cybertruck in 2021-2022, including expectations of volume 
production next year (Q1 2022) and being in production in the middle of next year (Q2 2022). Execution shifted into 
pilot/release-candidate phases through 2023 (alpha versions on a pilot line in Q1 2023; building release candidates on 
the final production line in Q2 2023) and management explicitly flagged challenges and uncertainty on reaching volume 
production. By 2023-2024, commentary focused on being in the early phase of ramp and ongoing ramp effects (Q3 2023, Q4 
2023, Q2 2024), with no evidence in the provided record that the initially stated mid/next-year timing was met at volume
scale.

Key quotes:
  - Obviously, those take priority over the Cybertruck, but we are moving into the beta phases of Cybertruck later this 
year, and we will be looking to ramp that in production and take it to Texas after Model Y is up and going.
  - So in order for Cybertruck and Semi to scale to volume that's meaningful for customer deliveries, we've got to solve
the chip shortage working with our suppliers.
  - Elon Musk -- Chief Executive Officer I think Cybertruck ramp will be difficult because it's such new architecture.
