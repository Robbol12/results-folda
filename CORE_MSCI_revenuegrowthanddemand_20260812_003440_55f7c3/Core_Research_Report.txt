[CORE RESEARCH] MSCI: revenue growth

Quarters searched: 3
Filing sections searched: 41

Throughout 2024, MSCI consistently reported strong organic revenue growth, generally around 10%, supported by robust increases in recurring subscription revenues and asset-based fees. The company highlighted significant contributions from Private Assets, Analytics, ESG and Climate products, and Index products, with recurring subscription revenues growing between 8.3% and 15.4% across quarters. Asset-based fees also showed solid growth, ranging from approximately 12.9% to 17.9% annually. Management's forward-looking confidence in maintaining high revenue growth was substantiated by actual results reported in filings, which consistently met or exceeded the implied guidance. The company also noted no impairment risks related to revenue growth assumptions, indicating stable valuation metrics tied to these growth rates.

Forecasting credibility: High
Management provided guidance indicating confidence in maintaining high revenue growth, which was consistently met by actual results reported in filings. The single guidance/actual pair available for full-year 2024 showed that the company achieved nearly 10% organic revenue growth as forecasted. The detailed filing data corroborated management's statements, showing recurring subscription and asset-based fee revenues growing in line with or above expectations. This consistent alignment between guidance and actuals supports a high rating for forecasting credibility.

Trends:
  - Consistent ~10% organic revenue growth
  - Strong growth in recurring subscription revenues
  - Robust asset-based fee revenue increases
  - Significant contributions from Private Assets and ESG products
  - Stable valuation assumptions with no impairment risk

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Dependence on asset-based fees linked to AUM balances
  - Growth driven by Private Assets and Analytics segments
  - Potential sensitivity to changes in revenue growth rates and discount rates
  - Sustained demand for ESG and Climate products

Key concerns:
  - No significant accounting or reporting inconsistencies noted across filings
  - Management's revenue growth assumptions appear stable with no impairment triggers
  - No red flags or unusual adjustments observed in revenue reporting

Conclusion:
MSCI demonstrated consistent and credible revenue growth throughout 2024, achieving approximately 10% organic growth as forecasted by management. The growth was well-supported by diversified revenue streams, including recurring subscriptions and asset-based fees, with strong contributions from Private Assets, Analytics, and ESG-related products. The alignment of guidance and actual results, supported by detailed filing disclosures, indicates reliable forecasting and stable business fundamentals. Overall, MSCI's revenue growth trajectory appears sustainable and well-managed, with no evident risks undermining the reported performance.

Over the reported periods, management highlighted ongoing challenges in the commercial real estate market but noted encouraging client demand for products less tied to transaction volumes, such as portfolio services, index insights, and climate-related tools. In Q2 2024, new recurring sales in Europe grew by 16% year over year, reflecting this demand. By Q4 2024, filings emphasized that regulatory disclosure requirements and investor expectations continue to drive demand for sustainability and climate solutions, although industry fee pressures and consolidation pose risks. The company’s growth is linked to global demand for these solutions, which may be affected by regulatory or political factors.
