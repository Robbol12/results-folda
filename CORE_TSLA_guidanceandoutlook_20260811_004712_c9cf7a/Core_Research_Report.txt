Over the course of 2022, management consistently provided capital expenditure outlook ranges in their filings, adjusting the expected range upward from $5.00-$7.00 billion early in the year to $6.00-$8.00 billion by mid-year and maintaining that range through Q3. The actual capital expenditures reported each quarter showed a steady increase, culminating in $7.16 billion for the full year 2022, which fell within the guided range. For 2023 and the following two fiscal years, management projected a further increase in capital expenditures, expecting $6.00-$8.00 billion in 2023 and $7.00-$9.00 billion thereafter. The outlook commentary also referenced ongoing impacts and easing of COVID-19 related challenges. However, no explicit forward guidance paired with actual results was provided for other outlook components beyond capital expenditures.

Therefore, it is not possible to assess management's forecasting credibility for the overall outlook. The assessment refrains from inferring credibility from general commentary or capital expenditure guidance alone, adhering strictly to the absence of paired guidance-actual data.

  - COVID-19 impact easing over time

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Capital expenditure management
  - COVID-19 pandemic impact and recovery

Key concerns:

  - Capital expenditure guidance ranges shifted upward during the year, reflecting possible adjustments to project scope or cost assumptions.
  - No accounting or reporting inconsistencies were noted in the capital expenditure disclosures.

Conclusion:
The outlook for capital expenditures was clearly communicated with ranges that were generally consistent with actual spending, indicating reasonable management visibility in this area. The company anticipates increased capital spending in 2023 and subsequent years, reflecting ongoing investment in projects despite easing COVID-19 challenges.
