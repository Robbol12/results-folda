Longitudinal Summary

Throughout 2024, production volumes showed a steady increase quarter over quarter despite early challenges such as the production ramp of the updated Model 3 at Fremont and shutdowns at Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg due to external disruptions. Cybertruck production ramped notably, reaching over 1,000 units per week by Q1 and continuing to scale. Management consistently provided guidance on production ramp timelines for new models and cell production improvements, with actual production figures from filings confirming growth from approximately 433,000 vehicles in Q1 to 1,773,000 vehicles by year-end. The company also highlighted ongoing development and pilot production for future models like Cybercab and Tesla Semi. Overall, production capacity expanded while navigating operational challenges and model transitions.

Forecasting Credibility: High

The two guidance/actual pairs available both show management met or exceeded their production expectations, with actual production figures from filings confirming steady growth aligned with guidance. The consistency between forward-looking statements and subsequent reported production volumes, especially the met guidance through Q2 and the transcript confirmation of production ramp progress, supports a high level of forecasting credibility. No significant misses were noted in the pairs provided.
