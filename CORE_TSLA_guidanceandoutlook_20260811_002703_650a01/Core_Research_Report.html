Longitudinal Summary

Over the periods reviewed, management provided very limited forward guidance, with a single explicit numeric target given in Q3 2020 for 500,000 deliveries in 2020. However, no subsequent guidance was provided for 2021 or beyond, and management explicitly stated in Q4 2020 that it was difficult to give guidance. The filings frequently referenced accounting standards updates and guidance related to credit losses and lease accounting, but these were regulatory or accounting guidance rather than forward-looking operational or financial guidance. Overall, the company appears cautious or constrained in providing formal forward guidance, often emphasizing assumptions and risks that could affect results.

Management acknowledges the difficulty in providing guidance due to uncertainties and risks. The filings focus more on accounting and regulatory guidance rather than operational forecasts.
