[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: 4680 cell production

VERDICT: Fulfilled (confidence: high)

Management initially guided toward volume 4680 production in 2021-2022, with expectations for volume production by mid/late 2022 (Q1 2022) and ramping to >1,000 cells/week by end of 2022 (Q2 2022). By Q4 2022 they reported achieving 1,000 a week, and subsequent quarters (Q2 2023, Q1 2024, Q2 2024) reported continued strong ramp and reaching >1K a week for Cybertruck, with the cell cost benefits tied to the original 4680 goals (Q2 2024). The evidence indicates the initiative was delivered and ramped over time rather than abandoned or merely piloted.
