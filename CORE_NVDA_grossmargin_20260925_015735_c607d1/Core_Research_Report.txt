[CORE RESEARCH] NVDA: gross margin

Quarters searched: 3
Filing sections searched: 87

Across FY2024 quarterly reporting, management guided gross margin upward each quarter (GAAP and non-GAAP) while actuals trended up but generally landed below the guided GAAP level and slightly below the non-GAAP level (Q1–Q3: 64.6% vs 68.6% guided GAAP; 70.1% vs 71.5% guided GAAP; 74.0% vs 74.5% guided GAAP). The gap narrowed by Q3, with actual GAAP 74% matching the qualitative drivers cited (data center mix and lower net inventory reserves, including an Ampere-related benefit). In FY2024 full-year, filings attribute gross margin expansion to data center growth and lower net inventory provisions, with GAAP gross margin reported at 72.7% for FY2024 versus 56.9% for FY2023. By FY2025 filings, gross margin trajectory remained strong in aggregate (mid-70s expectation; Q2/Q3 showing solid realized margins), but management disclosed an offsetting risk from inventory provisions tied to low-yielding Blackwell materials.

Conclusion:
Gross margin improved materially over the period, from 56.9% in FY2023 to 72.7% in FY2024, according to the filings. The quarterly evidence shows continued strengthening in FY2024 (GAAP gross margin rising from 64.6% in Q1 to 70.1% in Q2 and 74.0% in Q3), with management attributing the gains to data center mix and lower net inventory reserves, including a one-percentage-point Ampere-related benefit. In FY2025, filings show gross margins of 78.4% (Q1) and 75.1% (Q2), and 75.8% (Q3), while also warning that inventory provisions for low-yielding Blackwell material may further pressure margins. Overall, gross margin performance is supported by mix and inventory reserve dynamics, with product-ramp-related inventory provisions representing the recurring downside risk.
