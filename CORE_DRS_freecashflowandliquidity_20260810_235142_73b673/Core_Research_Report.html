Longitudinal Summary

Throughout 2023, the company's free cash flow showed a pattern of negative usage in the first three quarters, with increasing cash outflows reported in Q1, Q2, and Q3. Specifically, free cash flow usage worsened from $(346) million in Q1 to $(356) million in the first half and $(335) million for the nine months ended September 30, 2023. However, by year-end, the company reported a significant turnaround, with free cash flow increasing by $85 million to a positive $159 million for the full year 2023, compared to $74 million in 2022. The definition of free cash flow evolved slightly, with the year-end filing including dividends from investments and tax payments on disposals in the calculation. Management provided forward guidance only once in Q2 2023, targeting free operating cash flow of €600 million for the full year, but no direct guidance-actual pairs for free cash flow were available.

Forecasting Credibility: None

There are zero guidance versus actual pairs specifically for free cash flow, as management rarely provides explicit forward guidance on this metric. The single forward-looking statement in Q2 2023 referenced free operating cash flow targets but did not provide a precise figure for free cash flow itself, and no subsequent actuals were directly compared to that guidance. Therefore, it is not possible to assess forecasting credibility for free cash flow based on the available data.

Conclusion

Free cash flow was negative and deteriorated through the first three quarters of 2023, reflecting increased cash usage primarily driven by operating activities and capital expenditures. However, the company achieved a notable turnaround by year-end, reporting positive free cash flow of $159 million, more than doubling the prior year's $74 million. The inclusion of dividends from investments and tax payments on disposals in the year-end free cash flow calculation suggests a broader measure was adopted, which may partly explain the improvement. Despite management's verbal guidance on free operating cash flow targets, no explicit free cash flow guidance-actual pairs exist, preventing an assessment of forecasting reliability. Overall, the evidence indicates improving cash generation capacity by year-end, but forecasting credibility for free cash flow remains unassessed due to lack of direct guidance.
