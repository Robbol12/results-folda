Longitudinal Summary

Across Q2 2024 and Q3 2024, filings attributed regional year-over-year movements primarily to changes in iPhone net sales: Europe and Greater China were pressured in the quarter (Q2), while Japan and Rest of Asia Pacific saw iPhone-led strength in Q3. In Q4 2024, management highlighted a specific iPhone growth outcome—September-quarter iPhone revenues of $46.2B, up 6% year over year with growth in every geographic segment—yet the subsequent filing characterization for full-year 2024 indicated iPhone net sales were relatively flat versus 2023. For Q1 2025, the filings again showed iPhone as the main driver of regional divergence, with Japan up on higher iPhone net sales and Greater China down due to lower iPhone net sales. Overall, the period reflects quarter-to-quarter and region-specific volatility rather than a consistent iPhone revenue growth trend at the total-year level.
