[CORE RESEARCH] AAPL: iPhone revenue growth

Quarters searched: 1
Filing sections searched: 84

Across Q2 2024 and Q3 2024, filings attributed regional year-over-year movements primarily to changes in iPhone net sales: Europe and Greater China were pressured in the quarter (Q2), while Japan and Rest of Asia Pacific saw iPhone-led strength in Q3. In Q4 2024, management highlighted a specific iPhone growth outcome—September-quarter iPhone revenues of $46.2B, up 6% year over year with growth in every geographic segment—yet the subsequent filing characterization for full-year 2024 indicated iPhone net sales were relatively flat versus 2023. For Q1 2025, the filings again showed iPhone as the main driver of regional divergence, with Japan up on higher iPhone net sales and Greater China down due to lower iPhone net sales. Overall, the period reflects quarter-to-quarter and region-specific volatility rather than a consistent iPhone revenue growth trend at the total-year level.

  - Q3 2024: iPhone-led strength in Japan and Rest of Asia Pacific
  - Full-year 2024: iPhone net sales described as relatively flat vs 2023
  - Q1 2025: iPhone remains the key driver of regional divergence (Japan up, Greater China down)

Recurring themes/risks:

Key concerns:

  - Because no explicit iPhone revenue-growth guidance and corresponding actuals are provided, there is no measurable forecasting accuracy signal for iPhone revenue growth.

Conclusion:
Based on the provided filings and excerpts, iPhone revenue growth was not uniformly positive over the full period. While management reported the September quarter iPhone revenues at $46.2B, up 6% year over year (with growth in every geographic segment) in Q4 2024, the filings also state that iPhone net sales were relatively flat during 2024 compared to 2023. Additionally, regional quarterly results repeatedly cite iPhone as the primary driver of year-over-year changes—Europe and Greater China were pressured in Q2 2024, Japan and parts of Asia Pacific improved in Q3 2024, and Q1 2025 again showed Japan up on higher iPhone net sales while Greater China declined due to lower iPhone net sales. Therefore, iPhone revenue growth appears episodic and region-dependent rather than consistently growing across all periods.
