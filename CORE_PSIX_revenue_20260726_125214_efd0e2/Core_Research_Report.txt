Quarters searched: 4

Over the 2015 reporting periods, management consistently provided revenue guidance that was generally optimistic, projecting strong growth and record revenues. Early in the year, guidance anticipated nearly 30% revenue growth, with quarterly revenue targets set in ranges that were mostly met or slightly missed. Actual revenue growth was positive but often below the high-end guidance, with some quarters showing significant misses relative to initial expectations. The company faced a challenging competitive environment and implemented new revenue recognition standards, which were consistently disclosed. By year-end, revenue growth was solid but somewhat below the most aggressive forecasts, with a 12% increase reported for 2015 and cautious guidance for a slight decline in 2016 revenue.

Forecasting credibility: Medium
Management's revenue guidance was generally credible in that they often met or came close to their forecasted ranges, especially in quarterly revenue figures. However, there were notable misses, particularly in the mid-year periods where growth was less than initially projected, and the full-year revenue growth was below the nearly 30% target. The presence of four guidance/actual pairs with a mix of met and missed outcomes supports a medium rating, reflecting reasonable but not consistently precise forecasting.

Trends:
  - Revenue growth positive but below peak guidance
  - Quarterly revenue targets mostly met or slightly missed
  - Increasing revenue from acquisitions
  - Implementation of new revenue recognition standards
  - Cautious outlook for 2016 revenue decline

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Significant competition impacting revenue
  - Revenue recognition accounting changes
  - Dependence on industrial OEM and transportation markets
  - Acquisition contributions to revenue

Key concerns:
  - Revenue growth guidance was optimistic relative to actual results, with some quarters missing targets.
  - New revenue recognition standards were adopted, but no inconsistencies or restatements were noted across filings.
  - The company disclosed undelivered product revenue, which could affect timing of revenue recognition.

Conclusion:
Revenue grew steadily throughout 2015, with management initially guiding for nearly 30% growth and quarterly revenues in specific ranges. Actual results showed positive growth but fell short of the highest expectations, with a reported 12% increase to $389 million for the year. Quarterly revenues generally met or were close to guidance, though some mid-year guidance was missed. The company faced a competitive environment and adopted new revenue recognition standards without apparent reporting issues. Looking forward, management projected a modest revenue decline in 2016, reflecting a more cautious outlook after a strong but somewhat uneven 2015.
