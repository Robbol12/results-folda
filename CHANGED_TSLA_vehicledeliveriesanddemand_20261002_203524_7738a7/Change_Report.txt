Change detected: True

In Q3, management framed delivery strength as broad-based and supported by excitement around the newly launched Model Y and regional momentum, with no explicit mention of a demand pull-forward from specific US policy/credit factors. In Q4, management added more nuance: US demand was pulled forward into Q4 from a prior surge in Q3 ahead of a “consumer credit cliff,” and they also highlighted record deliveries in specific smaller countries alongside strength in APAC/EMEA. The reported delivery environment also shifted in emphasis from “pace of deliveries” tied to new Model Y launches (Q3) to mix effects and backlog/outlook considerations (Q4).

Key quotes:
  - "The strength in deliveries was attributed to strong performance across all regions."
  - "On the autos front, while in Q3, we saw a surge in US demand before the higher consumer credit cliff, pulling in some demand from Q4."
  - "In other parts of the world, we saw an increase in demand leading to record deliveries in smaller countries like Malaysia, Norway, Poland, Saudi Arabia, and Taiwan, while continued strength in the rest of APAC and EMEA."
