Quarters searched: 4

Across 2023, revenue (net operating revenues) grew modestly year-over-year in each quarter, with management attributing gains primarily to volume growth and favorable pricing/mix, partially offset by commodity cost inflation, FX headwinds, and higher marketing/operating spend. In 2024, management increased its organic revenue growth outlook to a higher range (8–9% for Q1 update; 9–10% for Q2; ~10% for Q3) and continued to characterize organic growth as at the high end of the long-term growth algorithm. However, the filing-reported top-line results show slower growth in Q1 2024 (net operating revenues +3% Y/Y) than implied by the organic growth guidance, while 2024-end commentary states that the company delivered robust organic revenue growth as part of 2024 results. By Q4 2024, management’s stated expectations (5–6% organic revenue growth for the subsequent period) were not resolved in the provided evidence.

  - Several “increase in operating income” drivers (volume/pricing and FX/commodity offsets) are discussed, but they are not direct net revenue growth rates in the excerpts, so attribution could blur the distinction between revenue and operating income impacts.

[CORE RESEARCH] KO: Operating margin

Quarters searched: 4
Filing sections searched: 81

In 2023, the company’s reported consolidated operating margin declined from 25.4% in 2022 to 24.7% in 2023, with segment margins mixed (e.g., EMEA down 57.4% to 56.8%, North America up 23.9% to 26.4%). In 2024 through Q2, management consistently guided/communicated a “margin profile” strengthening and reported comparable operating margin expansion of ~60 bps (Q1) and ~120 bps (Q2). Despite these incremental comparable operating margin improvements, the full-year reported consolidated operating margin fell from 24.7% (2023) to 21.2% (2024), and operating income declined from $11,311 million (2023) to $9,992 million (2024). The divergence suggests that “comparable operating margin expansion” discussed in quarterly commentary did not fully translate into improved reported consolidated operating margin for the full year, likely affected by non-comparable items and other charges referenced in the filings (e.g., contingent consideration remeasurement and FX impacts).

Because there are no countable guidance-versus-actual comparisons for operating margin in the provided evidence, forecasting credibility cannot be assessed.

Trends:
  - Reported consolidated operating margin decreased: 25.4% (2022) -> 24.7% (2023) -> 21.2% (2024)
  - Quarterly comparable operating margin showed sequential expansion in 2024: ~60 bps (Q1), ~120 bps (Q2), ~100 bps (Q3), ~80 bps (Q4)
  - Reported operating income declined in 2024 vs 2023: $9,992m vs $11,311m

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Comparable margin improvement offset by currency headwinds mentioned in quarterly commentary
  - Non-comparable items/charges referenced in filings (contingent consideration remeasurement; unfavorable foreign currency impacts) influencing reported results

Key concerns:
  - Potential mismatch between “comparable operating margin expansion” (quarterly basis) and “reported consolidated operating margin” (full-year) which declined materially in 2024 (24.7% to 21.2%).
  - Filings attribute increased operating losses/charges in both 2023 and 2024 to contingent consideration fair value remeasurement and foreign currency impacts—factors that may depress reported operating margin even if comparable margins improve.

Conclusion:
Operating margin (reported, consolidated) deteriorated over the period. The company reported consolidated operating margin of 25.4% in 2022 and 24.7% in 2023, but then fell to 21.2% in 2024. This decline aligns with operating income decreasing from $11,311 million in 2023 to $9,992 million in 2024. While management highlighted quarterly comparable operating margin expansion in 2024 (e.g., ~60 bps in Q1 and ~120 bps in Q2), the full-year reported operating margin decline indicates that comparable improvement did not translate into improved reported consolidated operating margin for 2024.
