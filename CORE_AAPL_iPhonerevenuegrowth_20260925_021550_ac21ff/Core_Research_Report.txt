Quarters searched: 1

Across 2Q–3Q 2024, the filings indicate iPhone net sales were “relatively flat” year over year in aggregate, while declines in Greater China and mixed results across geographies were cited as drivers (e.g., Greater China down due to lower iPhone net sales, with Japan up due to higher iPhone net sales). Through 9M 2024, the same pattern persists: Europe and Americas benefited from iPhone upswings offset by weakness elsewhere, but overall iPhone remained “relatively flat” versus 2023. In 1Q 2025, filings show Europe and Japan up year over year due to higher iPhone net sales, while Greater China remains down due to lower iPhone net sales.
