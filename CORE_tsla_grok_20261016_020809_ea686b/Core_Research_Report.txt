What the sources show:
Across Q2 2024 and Q3 2024, management consistently discussed integrating Grok into Tesla’s in-vehicle software ecosystem, framing it as part of broader efforts to expand what is available in the car on-screen. In Q2 2024, management stated there are “opportunities to integrate Grok into Tesla's software,” and linked Tesla’s learning from xAI to progress in full self-driving and a new Tesla data center, while noting that any additional xAI investing would require shareholder approval. In Q3 2024, management responded to a question about when Tesla would incorporate X and Grok across all vehicles by emphasizing ongoing expansion of in-car capabilities (screens, browser functionality) and the idea that an autonomous system should be able to do anything, including asking AI questions. Over the period, the emphasis shifts from a specific “opportunities” statement about Grok integration (Q2) to broader product/platform vision for expanding access to content and AI in the vehicle (Q3), without providing a concrete timeline. The disclosed facts remain qualitative, with no new quantitative performance metrics or detailed integration milestones presented in the excerpts.

  - Both periods provide directional statements but no measurable milestones, adoption rates, or technical delivery details for Grok specifically.
  - No stated schedule for incorporating Grok into all vehicles
