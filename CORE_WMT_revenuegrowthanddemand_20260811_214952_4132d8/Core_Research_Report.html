Longitudinal Summary

Over the reported periods in 2022, revenue growth showed a consistent upward trend with management highlighting strong constant currency revenue growth and improving underlying business trends. Actual reported figures from filings confirm steady increases in comparable sales and net sales across Walmart U.S. segments, with growth rates generally rising from Q1 through Q4. Inflation and increased average ticket sizes contributed to growth, alongside digital sales expansion and recovery from pandemic-related disruptions. Operating expenses increased somewhat but were partially offset by strong sales growth and lower COVID-19 costs.

Conclusion

Revenue growth in 2022 was robust and consistent, with comparable sales and net sales increasing steadily each quarter. Inflation and higher average ticket sizes were key drivers, alongside digital sales expansion and recovery from pandemic-related disruptions. Despite rising operating expenses, the company managed to grow revenue significantly, as evidenced by a 17% increase in revenue since fiscal 2021 excluding divestitures. However, the absence of explicit forward guidance on revenue growth means that management's ability to forecast this metric cannot be assessed from the available data.
