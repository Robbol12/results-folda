THE ANSWER

What the sources show

Across Q1 through Q4 2025, Services net sales consistently increased year-over-year, with management attributing growth primarily to higher net sales from advertising, the App Store, and cloud services. Services gross margin also rose in each quarter/period, and the gross margin percentage generally increased, supported mainly by a different services mix, with offsets from higher services costs. In Q2 and Q3, the filings further specify that currency movements (weakness in foreign currencies relative to the U.S. dollar) and higher costs affected gross margin percentage performance. In Q3, management highlighted a very strong Services quarter and an all-time record, and noted sequential acceleration across most categories including cloud services, reinforcing that growth was not only year-over-year but also strengthening sequentially. Over the full fiscal year, the company reiterated that Services net sales and gross margin increased versus 2024 primarily due to higher net sales from the same key services categories and a different mix, partially offset by higher costs.

Watch points

  • Gross margin percentage drivers rely heavily on 'different mix of services' and 'partially offset by higher Services costs,' implying profitability could be sensitive to cost inflation and mix shifts.
  • Currency effects are only explicitly mentioned in Q2/H1 (weakness in foreign currencies vs. U.S. dollar), suggesting some variability in reported margin performance that may not be fully controlled.
  • Growth driven primarily by advertising, App Store, and cloud services
  • Services mix changes supported gross margin percentage
  • Higher services costs partially offset gross margin percentage gains
  • Foreign currency weakness (vs. U.S. dollar) impacted gross margin percentage in Q2/H1
