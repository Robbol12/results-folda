[PROMISE TRACKING] NVDA: Blackwell production ramp

VERDICT: Fulfilled (confidence: medium)

Management initially discussed Blackwell availability at launch (Q1 2025) but also framed production ramp timing as starting in Q4 and continuing into FY26 (Q2 2025). By Q3 2025 they claimed Blackwell production was “in full steam,” and by Q4 2025 they reported the Blackwell ramp had commenced, that production was across multiple configurations, and that they delivered $11B of Blackwell revenue. Taken together, the evidence suggests the ramp plan began as scheduled and reached scale by Q4 2025, although some earlier ramp details are still described ambiguously.

Key quotes:
  - “Blackwell will be available in over 100 OEM and ODM systems at launch, more than double the number of Hoppers launched and representing every major computer maker in the world.” (NVDA_Q1_2025)
  - “Blackwell production ramp is scheduled to begin in the fourth quarter and continue into fiscal year '26.” (NVDA_Q2_2025)
  - And we successfully and incredibly ramped up Grace Blackwell, delivering some $11 billion of revenues last quarter.
