Conclusion:
Across the provided periods, “data” is treated mainly as (1) an input to AI development and scaling (including training compute and AI infrastructure/data centers) and (2) a driver of valuation/estimate sensitivity (e.g., impacts of accumulating data on resale value assumptions and stock-based awards valuation).
