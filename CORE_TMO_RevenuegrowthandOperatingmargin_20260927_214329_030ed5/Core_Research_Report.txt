[CORE RESEARCH] TMO: Revenue growth

Quarters searched: 3
Filing sections searched: 71

Across 2H 2023 into 2024, management positioned revenue growth as resilient but modest, citing core organic revenue growth near ~1% and expecting improving quarterly trends through the year. In Q3 2023, multiple FY2024 framing statements were met (including guidance that FY2024 core organic growth would be ~similar to 2023). In Q4 2023, the full-year 2024 organic revenue range guidance was partially missed on the “minus 1% to positive 1%” framing (per the verdict), though the company indicated improving sequential quarters and reported results consistent with improvement in the second half. In 2024, Q2 showed better-than-previously-assumed performance (core organic growth “a little over 0.5 percentage point ahead”), while management maintained a rounded FY2024 range and provided higher Q3 modeling assumptions; Q3 2024 results were not provided in the evidence set, leaving that pair unresolved.

Forecasting credibility: Medium
Out of 8 guidance/actual pairs with a verdict, management Met/Exceeded in 6 and Missed in 1, with 1 left Unresolved. This “mostly Met” pattern is consistent with credible forecasting for the revenue-growth trajectory, though at least one notable miss occurred around the FY2024 organic growth framing in Q4 2023. The unresolved item (Q3 2024 outcome) limits confidence in near-term precision, but the majority of resolved pairs were met.

Trends:
  - Core organic revenue growth guided around ~1% with an improving quarterly cadence
  - Q2 2024 core organic growth outperformed the prior guide assumption by ~0.5 percentage point
  - COVID-19 related demand headwinds and China macro pressure repeatedly referenced as drivers of slower regional growth

Recurring themes/risks:
  - COVID-19 product demand runoff
  - Challenging macroeconomic environment and low activity in China
  - Revenue growth sensitivity to organic demand vs. acquisitions (reported revenue includes acquisition contributions)
  - Guidance expressed as core organic growth (excluding acquisitions/divestitures and currency effects) to isolate underlying demand

Key concerns:
  - Q4 2023 guidance for FY2024 core organic revenue growth in the range of minus 1% to positive 1% is marked as a miss in the guidance/actual verdict set, indicating at least one period where the communicated range did not track the eventual outcome well (exact FY2024 result not provided in the excerpts).
  - One guidance item tied to “relative to the midpoint…model Q3…1% higher than we delivered in Q2” remains unresolved due to missing Q3 2024 outcome data in the provided evidence.
  - Acquisition-related contributions were explicitly assumed for reported revenue growth in Q3 2023, but the pair “~$300 million to reported revenue growth for the year” was marked missed, suggesting reported (as opposed to core organic) revenue forecasting incorporating deal impact was less reliable.

Conclusion:
Revenue growth evolved from a modest, core-organic baseline (~1% core organic growth) with an expectation of sequential improvement in 2H 2023 into early/mid-2024 where management maintained the rounded FY2024 organic growth band while reporting better-than-expected Q2 organic performance. In Q3 2023, guidance that FY2024 core organic growth would be similar to 2023 (~1%) was met, and Q3 2023 also reported 1% core organic growth. In Q4 2023, the guidance framing for FY2024 core organic growth was assessed as missed in the pair verdict set, though management also guided for improvement each quarter and referenced market conditions playing out as expected. In Q2 2024, management reported core organic revenue growth “a little over 0.5 percentage point ahead” of the prior Q2 guide assumption (with the pair marked met), supporting that underlying demand trended better than expected at least by that point in the year.

[CORE RESEARCH] TMO: Operating margin

Quarters searched: 3
Filing sections searched: 71

Across 2023, GAAP operating margin benefited at quarter level from productivity and price realization, while also being pressured by lower COVID-19 related volumes and restructuring charges. In Q3 2023, management guided to an FY 2023 adjusted operating margin of 22.9% and later resolved that guidance as Exceeded in the Q4 2023 reporting, but other margin-related guidance in adjusted operating income dollars did not resolve in-line (Missed). In Q4 2023, the company reiterated FY 2023 adjusted operating margin of 22.9% as in line with prior guide, while Q4 2023 guidance for 2024 adjusted operating margin (22.3%–22.8%) was later missed. In 2024, GAAP and adjusted operating margins were described as improving early in the year due to productivity gains, but by Q2 2024 at least one adjusted operating margin measure was materially below the prior-year quarter, and full-year 2024 was described as decreasing on GAAP largely due to business mix and strategic investments (partially offset by lower amortization expense).

Forecasting credibility: Medium
Out of 3 guidance/actual pairs with verdicts, management demonstrated credibility with 2 outcomes resolving as Exceeded (Q3 2023 FY adjusted operating margin 22.9%) and 1 outcome resolving as Missed (2024 adjusted operating margin range 22.3%–22.8%). One additional margin-related guidance point (Q1 slightly under 21%) is explicitly marked unresolved in the provided evidence, but it is not included in the 3-pair verdict count. Overall, the limited sample shows mixed precision across forward periods, with better accuracy for the FY 2023 adjusted margin than for the 2024 margin range.

Trends:
  - GAAP operating income margin affected by lower COVID-19 volumes and restructuring charges in 2023
  - Productivity improvements and price realization supported adjusted operating margins in Q3 2023
  - FY 2023 adjusted operating margin target around 22.9% ultimately resolved higher than guided/expected within the pair framework
  - 2024 GAAP operating margin described as decreasing despite productivity gains, with lower amortization offsetting
  - Adjusted operating margin performance in 2024 shows internal variability by quarter/measure (e.g., Q2 below prior-year for one measure)

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Revenue headwinds from COVID-19 related demand variability
  - Restructuring/facility consolidation charges impacting GAAP operating margin
  - Strategic investments and unfavorable business mix pressuring margins
  - Productivity improvements partially offsetting operational headwinds
  - Amortization expense levels (including lower amortization in 2024) affecting GAAP margin

Key concerns:
  - GAAP operating margin statements are repeatedly framed as being impacted by restructuring charges and fixed intangible amortization, making GAAP margin less comparable across periods versus adjusted margin.
  - The Q2 2024 evidence shows multiple adjusted operating margin figures (e.g., 12.9% and 24.6% and 22.3% and 26.7% in the transcript), which indicates sensitivity to how margin is defined/measured; this complicates interpretation of “operating margin” without a single consistent metric.
  - One Q1 2024 margin guidance point is marked Unresolved (slightly under 21%), limiting confidence in short-cycle margin forecasting based on the provided dataset.

Conclusion:
Operating margin evolved from being pressured in 2023 by lower COVID-19 related revenue and restructuring/intangible amortization effects toward quarters where productivity and price realization improved margins (e.g., Q3 2023 GAAP operating income margin described as up due to productivity and price realization, partially offset by lower COVID-19 revenue). For adjusted operating margin, management guided to 22.9% for FY 2023 and the later reported outcome resolved as Exceeded within the guidance/actual pair framework, with GAAP operating income margin in Q3 2023 at 17.6% versus 16.0% and a full-year GAAP operating income margin of 16.0% versus 18.7% (filing). For 2024, the company guided adjusted operating income margins in a 22.3%–22.8% range, but that was resolved as Missed, and in 2024 the filing narrative indicates GAAP operating margin decreased due to unfavorable business mix and strategic investments, partially offset by lower amortization expense.
