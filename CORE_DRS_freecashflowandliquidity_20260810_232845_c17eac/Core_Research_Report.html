Longitudinal Summary

Over the reported periods, free cash flow consistently showed negative values in the quarterly filings for 2022, with usage increasing from $(262) million in Q1 2021 to $(268) million in Q1 2022, and further deteriorating to $(264) million by mid-year and $(262) million by Q3 2022. The annual figures reveal a decrease in free cash flow from $122 million in 2021 to $74 million in 2022. Management highlighted improvements in free operating cash flow in 2022, noting a step-up from €209 million to €539 million and a cash conversion rate of 55%, with an adjusted figure excluding Aerostructures of about €300 million higher. Guidance was provided on cumulative free cash flow targets (€3 billion by 2025) and net debt reduction, but no explicit forward guidance on free cash flow for interim periods was given.

Conclusion

Free cash flow has been negative on a quarterly basis throughout 2022, with a declining trend compared to 2021. However, management reported a significant improvement in free operating cash flow for the full year 2022, more than doubling from the prior year, and set a cumulative free cash flow target of €3 billion by 2025. Despite these positive signals, the absence of explicit forward guidance on free cash flow in the interim periods means forecasting credibility cannot be evaluated. The company appears focused on improving cash flow quality and conversion rates, which supports a cautiously optimistic outlook on free cash flow generation over the medium term.
