TOPIC: production
======================================================================

[CORE RESEARCH] PSIX: production

Quarters searched: 4
Filing sections searched: 31

Over the 2015 reporting periods, production was discussed primarily in the context of capacity expansion and associated inefficiencies. Early in the year, the company highlighted risks related to production facility operability and noted ongoing customer production support efforts. By Q3 and Q4, the company reported increased production spending and inefficiencies, which negatively impacted gross profit despite efforts to ramp up production capacity, particularly for proprietary engines like the 8.8 and 4.3 models. The company expected to triple production capacity in 2016, indicating a strategic focus on scaling manufacturing capabilities.

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance versus actual pairs were provided for production metrics, so it is not possible to assess management's forecasting credibility on this topic. The available commentary and filings focus on qualitative descriptions and capacity targets without precise forward guidance or subsequent actual comparisons.

Trends:
  - Increasing production capacity
  - Rising production spending
  - Production inefficiencies impacting gross profit
  - Focus on proprietary engine production ramp-up

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Production facility operability risk
  - Production inefficiencies
  - Competitive pricing pressures
  - Customer production support linkage

Key concerns:
  - Gross profit declines linked to higher production spending and inefficiencies suggest operational challenges during capacity expansion.
  - No quantitative production guidance or actual output figures were disclosed, limiting transparency on production performance.
  - Potential risk if production facilities become inoperable, as repeatedly noted in filings.

Conclusion:
The company is actively investing in expanding production capacity, particularly for its proprietary engines, with plans to significantly increase output in 2016. However, this expansion has been accompanied by production inefficiencies and higher spending, which have negatively affected gross profit margins. While management emphasizes customer production support and the strategic importance of production facilities, the lack of specific production guidance and actual output data limits the ability to fully evaluate production performance. Overall, production is a critical operational focus but remains a source of cost pressure and risk during this growth phase.
