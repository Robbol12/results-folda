[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: 4680 cell production

VERDICT: Fulfilled (confidence: medium)

Management initially suggested 4680 volume production would be achieved in 2021/2022 (Q1 2021) and later expected to be 
in volume production by late Q3/Q4 2022 (Q1 2022). By Q4 2021 they referenced delivering first 4680 vehicles that 
quarter, and by Q2 2022 they were not dependent on 4680 for near-term vehicle production while planning to ramp to over 
1,000 cells/week by end of 2022. Evidence then shows execution: they achieved 1,000 a week in Q4 2022, reported strong 
ramp progress and output milestones in 2022-2024 (Q2/Q4 2023, Q1/Q2 2024), including cost improvements and being on 
track for additional manufacturing advances in Q4 2024 (dry cathode).

Key quotes:
  - “We expect to ramp total 4680 production to exceed 1,000 per week by the end of the year, hopefully before – well 
before.”
  - But overall, I think, we still feel quite optimistic about achieving volume production of the 4680 next year.
  - 4680 output is not a risk to achieving 1.5 million vehicles produced this year, but it would become a risk next year
if we do not solve volume production by early 2023, but we’re highly confident of doing so.
