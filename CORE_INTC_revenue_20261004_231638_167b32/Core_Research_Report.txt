Across 1H 2024, Intel reported total revenue of $12.7 billion in Q1 (up $1.0 billion or 9% vs. Q1 2023) supported by higher CCG (+31%) and DCAI (+5%), though NEX revenue declined (−8%). In Q2 2024, revenue components remained mixed: desktop revenue was up (desktop revenue $2.5 billion, +$157 million vs. Q2 2023) while other revenue was $403 million (down $111 million vs. Q2 2023), reflecting ongoing legacy business exits. In Q3 2024, results shifted materially downward as total revenue was $13.3 billion (down $874 million vs. Q3 2023), driven by declines in CCG (−7%), Altera® (−44%), and external Intel Foundry revenue (−79%), partially offset by DCAI (+9%) and NEX revenue rising to $1.5 billion (+$61 million). By full-year 2024, operating segment revenue summary showed revenue of $17.5 billion (down $1.4 billion vs. 2023) and “All other revenue” of $3.8 billion (down $1.8 billion vs. 2023), indicating the quarterly declines translated into weaker full-year revenue.
