Over the course of 2021, the company's income showed a strong upward trajectory, with net income attributable to common stockholders increasing significantly each quarter compared to the prior year. Favorable changes in other income (expense), primarily driven by foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations and mark-to-market remeasurement of interest rate swaps, contributed positively to income. The company reported a cumulative GAAP net income of $5.52 billion for the full year 2021, marking a substantial improvement from the previous year and turning accumulated profitability positive. These results were consistently supported by detailed filing disclosures each quarter, highlighting both operational income and tax provisions.

Conclusion:
Income for the company in 2021 demonstrated robust growth, with net income attributable to common stockholders rising from $438 million in Q1 to a full-year total of $5.52 billion. This growth was supported by operational improvements and favorable external factors like currency exchange rates and interest rate swap valuations. Despite the strong reported income, management did not provide forward guidance on income, so forecasting credibility cannot be evaluated. The consistent filing disclosures provide reliable and detailed income data, showing a clear positive trend in profitability throughout the year.
