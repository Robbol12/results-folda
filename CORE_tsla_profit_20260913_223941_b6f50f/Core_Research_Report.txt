[CORE RESEARCH] TSLA: profit

Quarters searched: 8
Filing sections searched: 189

Over the periods from Q1 2024 through Q4 2025, Tesla's profit narrative consistently highlighted record or improving profitability, particularly in the energy storage segment. Management frequently emphasized record gross profits and deployments in energy storage, with the Megapack and Powerwall contributing significantly. Despite tariff headwinds and a challenging automotive environment, Tesla maintained profitability and even set new financial records in later quarters. The filings introduced updated segment disclosure requirements and noted substantive profit-sharing arrangements affecting reported profits. By Q4 2025, Tesla reported strong revenue growth and record gross profits, confirming the positive trajectory in profitability.

Forecasting credibility: High
Tesla's management consistently provided guidance or commentary that aligned with or was supported by actual results, especially regarding record profitability in the energy business. The single guidance/actual pair available (Q4 2025) was met, reinforcing credibility. The detailed filing disclosures and repeated references to record profits and deployments across multiple quarters further support the reliability of management's profit forecasts.

Trends:
  - record energy storage profitability growth
  - increasing gross profit in energy segment
  - tariff impacts acknowledged but managed
  - consistent record-setting financial metrics
  - improving service and other segment profitability

Recurring themes/risks:
  - tariff and legislative headwinds
  - cost reduction and investment balance
  - profit-sharing arrangements affecting reporting
  - competitive automotive environment challenges

Key concerns:
  - The introduction of new accounting standards (ASU) and profit-sharing arrangements may complicate profit reporting and comparability across periods.
  - Tariff impacts are repeatedly noted as a risk to profitability, though management claims some mitigation advantages.
  - Some actual profit figures are only qualitatively described without precise numeric guidance, limiting granular forecasting assessment.

Conclusion:
Tesla demonstrated strong and improving profitability over the analyzed periods, particularly driven by record energy storage deployments and gross profits. Management's optimistic guidance about profitability was substantiated by actual results, including record gross profits and revenue growth by Q4 2025. While tariff risks and accounting complexities exist, Tesla's ability to set new financial records and maintain profitability in a challenging environment supports a positive profit outlook. The high forecasting credibility rating reflects management's consistent alignment of guidance with outcomes.
