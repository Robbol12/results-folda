Quarters compared: TSLA_Q3_2025, TSLA_Q4_2025

Management reported a significant improvement in automotive margins excluding credits, rising from 15.4% in Q3 to 17.9% 
in Q4, despite a 16% decline in deliveries. The Q4 commentary also introduced the transition to a subscription-based 
model for Full Self-Driving (FSD), which is expected to impact automotive margins negatively in the short term. 
Additionally, while energy storage margins remained strong with record deployments and gross profit in both quarters, Q4
emphasized record annual energy revenue growth and flagged margin and supply chain pressures for the year ahead.
