[CORE RESEARCH] ADBE: revenue

Quarters searched: 2
Filing sections searched: 36

Over the fiscal 2024 periods, Adobe's revenue showed consistent year-over-year growth across its Digital Media and Digital Experience segments, with Digital Media revenue increasing from $3.40 billion in Q1 2023 to $3.91 billion in Q2 2024 and $4.00 billion in Q3 2024, and Digital Experience revenue growing from $1.18 billion in Q1 2023 to $1.33 billion in Q2 2024 and $1.35 billion in Q3 2024. Management provided guidance for quarterly and full-year revenue targets, with some quarters missing the guidance range (Q3 and Q4 2024) while the full fiscal year 2024 revenue target was exceeded. Subscription revenue also showed steady growth, supporting the overall revenue increases. The company maintained consistent revenue recognition policies throughout the periods. Guidance for fiscal 2025 was provided but remains unresolved as actual results are not yet available.

Forecasting credibility: Medium
Adobe's management demonstrated a mixed forecasting record with two misses in quarterly revenue guidance (Q3 and Q4 2024) but an exceeded full-year 2024 revenue target. The presence of both misses and an exceeded target suggests some variability in forecasting accuracy, possibly due to external factors such as foreign exchange impacts and subscription model transitions. The use of filing-sourced actuals provides reliable verification of results, supporting a balanced view of credibility.

Trends:
  - Consistent year-over-year revenue growth
  - Increasing subscription revenue
  - Quarterly revenue guidance occasionally missed
  - Full-year revenue guidance exceeded
  - Steady growth in Digital Media and Digital Experience segments

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Impact of foreign exchange on revenue
  - Transition from perpetual to subscription offerings
  - Reliance on time-and-materials and fixed-fee contract revenue recognition

Key concerns:
  - Two quarterly revenue guidance misses (Q3 and Q4 2024) indicate some forecasting challenges.
  - Guidance for fiscal 2025 remains unresolved, limiting forward visibility.
  - No significant accounting or reporting changes noted, but the impact of subscription transition and FX headwinds could complicate future forecasting.

Conclusion:
Adobe's revenue demonstrated steady growth throughout fiscal 2024, with Digital Media and Digital Experience segments both contributing to increases. Subscription revenue growth was a key driver, rising to $4.86 billion in fiscal 2024 from $4.33 billion in fiscal 2023. While quarterly revenue guidance was occasionally missed, the company exceeded its full-year revenue target of $21.40 to $21.50 billion by reporting $21.51 billion, reflecting strong overall performance. The consistent revenue recognition approach and detailed segment reporting provide transparency. However, some forecasting variability and external factors such as foreign exchange and subscription model shifts present ongoing challenges for revenue predictability.
