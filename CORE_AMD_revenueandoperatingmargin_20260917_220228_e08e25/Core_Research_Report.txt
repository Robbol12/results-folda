[CORE RESEARCH] AMD: revenue

Quarters searched: 3
Filing sections searched: 44

Over the course of 2024, the company demonstrated consistent revenue growth driven primarily by strong performance in the Data Center and Client segments, with total net revenue increasing 14% year over year to $25.8 billion. Quarterly results showed steady increases in Data Center revenue, with notable growth percentages of 80%, 115%, and 107% in Q1, Q2, and Q3 respectively, while Client revenue also improved significantly. However, the Gaming and Embedded segments experienced declines, partially offsetting overall gains. Management's guidance generally anticipated growth, though some specific targets, particularly for Data Center GPU revenue, were missed. The company maintained a historical pattern of stronger second-half revenue, which was reflected in the Q4 record revenue of $7.7 billion, up 24% year over year.

Forecasting credibility: Medium
Management's revenue guidance was often directionally accurate, with most quarterly revenue figures meeting or exceeding expectations, especially in the Data Center and Client segments. However, some specific forward-looking targets, such as the Data Center GPU revenue exceeding $4.5 billion and later $5 billion for 2024, were missed, indicating occasional over-optimism. The presence of 13 guidance/actual pairs with a mix of met, exceeded, and missed outcomes supports a medium level of forecasting credibility overall.

Trends:
  - Strong Data Center revenue growth
  - Client segment recovery and growth
  - Declining Gaming and Embedded revenue
  - Higher revenue in second half of year
  - Increasing total net revenue year over year

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Market conditions impacting segment performance
  - Product transitions affecting revenue timing
  - Inventory corrections in PC supply chain
  - Seasonality in revenue patterns

Key concerns:
  - Missed guidance on Data Center GPU revenue targets suggests some forecasting optimism or market challenges.
  - Significant declines in Gaming and Embedded segments may pose risks to overall revenue stability.
  - No major accounting changes noted, but variable consideration under ASC 606 is acknowledged, which could affect revenue recognition.

Conclusion:
The company's revenue in 2024 showed robust growth, primarily fueled by the Data Center and Client segments, which consistently outperformed prior year periods and drove total net revenue to $25.8 billion, a 14% increase. Despite setbacks in Gaming and Embedded segments, the overall revenue trajectory was positive, with record quarterly revenues in Q4. Management's guidance was generally reliable, though some specific segment targets were missed, indicating moderate forecasting accuracy. The historical pattern of stronger second-half revenue held true, supporting expectations of continued growth in core segments.
