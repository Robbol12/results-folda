Over the course of 2023, the company demonstrated consistent revenue growth across multiple segments, with total operating revenues increasing steadily each quarter and culminating in a 12.5% increase for the full year. Recurring subscription revenues showed strong double-digit growth, particularly in Index, ESG and Climate, and Analytics products. Non-recurring revenues also contributed notably, especially in the fourth quarter with significant increases driven by fees for unlicensed content usage and licensed data products. Management highlighted organic revenue growth and recurring subscription run rate growth in their commentary, aligning with the filing data. The revenue growth trend was steady and broad-based across product lines throughout the year.

Non-recurring revenues also grew substantially, particularly in the fourth quarter, contributing to overall revenue expansion. Despite the absence of forward guidance on revenue growth, the company demonstrated solid execution and expansion across its key product lines, indicating healthy demand and effective revenue generation strategies.
