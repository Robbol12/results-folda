Over the periods from 2021 through early 2023, MacBook net sales generally showed growth driven by new product releases and chip upgrades, such as the M1 and M2-powered MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models. While MacBook Pro sales often offset declines in MacBook Air sales, there were periods of overall Mac net sales decreases, particularly in late 2022 and early 2023. New product introductions, including redesigned MacBook Air and updated MacBook Pro models, were consistently emphasized as key drivers of revenue performance.
