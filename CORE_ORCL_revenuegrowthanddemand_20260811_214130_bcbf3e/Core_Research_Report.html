Longitudinal Summary

Over the periods from Q1 2022 through Q4 2022, management consistently projected accelerating revenue growth, particularly driven by cloud business expansion. Early guidance anticipated mid-single digit total revenue growth with cloud revenues growing in the mid-20s percentage range. Actual results generally confirmed these trends, with cloud revenues growing around 22% and total revenue growth accelerating to 7-10% in constant currency by the end of fiscal 2022. Geographic contributions to growth shifted somewhat, with the Americas region consistently contributing the largest share, while EMEA and Asia Pacific showed mixed results. Despite some misses related to Cerner revenue contributions in early fiscal 2023, the overall pattern showed improving organic revenue growth and cloud business momentum.

Forecasting Credibility: High

Management's revenue growth guidance was largely met or exceeded across the nine guidance/actual pairs, especially regarding cloud revenue growth and total revenue acceleration. The actual cloud revenue growth figures of around 22% and total revenue growth reaching 7-10% in constant currency aligned well with the mid-single digit and mid-20s growth guidance. Although some misses occurred related to Cerner revenue contributions in early fiscal 2023, these did not materially undermine the overall credibility of revenue growth forecasts during fiscal 2022. The consistency of met or exceeded guidance supports a high rating for forecasting credibility.

Trends

Recurring Themes / Risks

Key Concerns

  • Some discrepancies in Cerner revenue contributions noted in early fiscal 2023 filings, which were marked as misses against guidance.
  • Regional revenue growth showed inconsistency, with Asia Pacific and EMEA regions experiencing partial offsets to growth in some quarters.
  • No significant accounting or reporting changes were noted that would affect revenue growth comparability.

Conclusion

Revenue growth showed a clear acceleration trend throughout fiscal 2022, driven primarily by the cloud business which grew in the low to mid-20% range, consistent with management's guidance. Total revenue growth improved from low single digits to approximately 7-10% in constant currency by year-end, reflecting successful execution on cloud and infrastructure expansion. Geographic contributions were dominated by the Americas, with EMEA and Asia Pacific regions showing mixed results but generally positive growth. Despite some misses related to Cerner revenue in early fiscal 2023, the overall evidence supports that revenue growth targets were credible and largely achieved, indicating strong momentum in the company's core growth drivers.
