[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: Cybertruck production timing

VERDICT: Delayed (confidence: high)

Management initially targeted volume production/production “next year” and “middle of next year” (Q1 2022 and Q2 2022), and later said they were building toward production/handover around end of Q3 2023 (Q1 2023). By Q2 2023 they were still building release candidates on the final production line, and in Q3 2023 management tempered expectations for volume production and explicitly said they would not reach the hoped-for output rate next year. There is no evidence in the provided calls that Cybertruck reached the promised volume production timing; instead, the timeline appears pushed out beyond the earlier targets.
