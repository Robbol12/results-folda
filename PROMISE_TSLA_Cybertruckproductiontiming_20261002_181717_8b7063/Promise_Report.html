[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: Cybertruck production timing

VERDICT: Delayed (confidence: high)

Management initially targeted volume production/production “next year” and “middle of next year” (Q1 2022 and Q2 2022), 
and later said they were building toward production/handover around end of Q3 2023 (Q1 2023). By Q2 2023 they were still
building release candidates on the final production line, and in Q3 2023 management tempered expectations for volume 
production and explicitly said they would not reach the hoped-for output rate next year. There is no evidence in the 
provided calls that Cybertruck reached the promised volume production timing; instead, the timeline appears pushed out 
beyond the earlier targets.

Key quotes:
  - “And we remain on track to reach volume production of the Cybertruck next year.” (TSLA_Q1_2022)
  - “With regard to our Cybertruck, we continue to build our release candidates of the Cybertruck on our final 
production line in Austin.” (TSLA_Q2_2023)
