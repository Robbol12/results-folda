In Q3 2025, management framed vehicle demand primarily through the lens of autonomy/FSD adoption, emphasizing that supervised FSD at scale would increase demand even though paid FSD penetration remained relatively small (~12% of the fleet). In Q4 2025, the framing shifted toward near-term geographic demand/delivery momentum and backlog, citing a US demand surge ahead of a consumer credit cliff and record deliveries in multiple smaller countries. The Q4 commentary also added that these markets did not yet have the latest version of FSD available, indicating demand strength despite limited FSD availability in those regions.
