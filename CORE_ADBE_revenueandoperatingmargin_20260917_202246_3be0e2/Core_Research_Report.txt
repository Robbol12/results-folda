[CORE RESEARCH] ADBE: revenue

Quarters searched: 2
Filing sections searched: 36

Over the fiscal 2024 periods, Adobe's revenue showed consistent year-over-year growth across its Digital Media and Digital Experience segments, with subscription revenue also increasing steadily. Management provided quarterly and full-year guidance that was generally close to actual results, with some misses in quarterly targets but an exceeded full-year revenue target. The company recognized revenues primarily as services were performed, with a shift towards subscription models noted. Guidance for fiscal 2025 was provided but remains unresolved in terms of actual results. Overall, revenue growth was driven by strong net new user growth and subscription uptake, despite some headwinds such as foreign exchange impacts.

Forecasting credibility: Medium
Adobe's management demonstrated a mixed forecasting record with two instances of exceeding revenue guidance and one instance of missing quarterly revenue targets. The full-year 2024 revenue guidance was exceeded, indicating reasonable accuracy over longer horizons, but quarterly guidance was sometimes missed, suggesting some short-term forecasting challenges. The presence of precise filing-sourced actuals supports the reliability of reported results, but the occasional misses reduce the overall forecasting credibility to medium.

Trends:
  - Consistent year-over-year revenue growth
  - Increasing subscription revenue
  - Quarterly revenue guidance occasionally missed
  - Full-year revenue guidance generally exceeded
  - Shift towards subscription-based revenue recognition

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Foreign exchange headwinds impacting revenue
  - Transition from perpetual to subscription offerings
  - Dependence on net new user growth
  - Revenue recognition tied to service performance

Key concerns:
  - Quarterly revenue guidance misses indicate some forecasting volatility in short term
  - No significant accounting changes noted, but revenue recognition depends on contract type which may affect comparability
  - Unresolved guidance for fiscal 2025 leaves some uncertainty about future forecasting accuracy

Conclusion:
Adobe's revenue grew steadily throughout fiscal 2024, with Digital Media and Digital Experience segments both showing double-digit year-over-year increases. Subscription revenue was a key growth driver, rising to $4.86 billion for the year, a 12% increase from fiscal 2023. While quarterly revenue guidance was sometimes missed, the company exceeded its full-year revenue guidance by reporting $21.51 billion against a target range of $21.40 to $21.50 billion. This demonstrates strong overall revenue performance and validates management's longer-term forecasting ability, despite some short-term variability. The company faces ongoing risks from foreign exchange and the transition to subscription models, but its revenue recognition policies appear consistent across periods.
