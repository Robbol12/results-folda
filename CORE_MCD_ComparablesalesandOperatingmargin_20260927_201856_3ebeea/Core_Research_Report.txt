[CORE RESEARCH] MCD: Comparable sales

Quarters searched: 2
Filing sections searched: 37

Across 2023, global comparable sales grew throughout the year (Q1 +12.6%, Q2 +11.7% for the quarter / +12.2% for six months, Q3 +8.8% for the quarter / +11.0% for nine months), with the U.S. and International Operated Markets segments also showing positive comps. In 2024, comparable sales decelerated meaningfully and turned modestly negative in several periods (Q1 +1.9% globally; Q2 -1.0% globally for the quarter), with segment-level declines in the U.S. and International Operated Markets. By Q3 2024, the global rate remained slightly positive/near-flat (+0.3% for U.S. comps, -2.1% for International Operated Markets comps; global comps not stated in the provided excerpt). In Q4 2024, management attributed U.S. comparable sales weakness (down 1.4%) to the E. coli outbreak tied to Quarter Pounders, indicating that episodic events drove comp volatility despite broader ongoing momentum initiatives.

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance/actual pairs for comparable sales were provided in the evidence set, so forecasting credibility cannot be assessed using the required guidance-vs-actual Met/Missed framework. As a result, the rating is set to null plainly due to the absence of comparable-sales forecasts paired with later reported outcomes.

Trends:
  - Strong comparable sales growth in 2023 (global comps consistently > +8% through Q3 2023)
  - Sharp deceleration in 2024 (global comps down to +1.9% in Q1 and -1.0% in Q2)
  - U.S. comps generally positive but weakened materially by Q4 2024 (-1.4% linked to E. coli outbreak)
  - International Operated Markets comps turned negative in late 2024 (e.g., Q3: -2.1% quarter comps)

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Currency translation and hyperinflation exclusions are applied to comparable sales measurement
  - Event risk impacting comps (E. coli outbreak linked to Quarter Pounders)
  - Segment divergence (U.S. vs International Operated Markets comps moving differently across quarters)

Key concerns:
  - No explicit comparable-sales forward guidance was provided in the evidence set for any quarter/period, preventing any guidance-vs-actual validation.
  - Some provided Q4 2024 commentary references management statements (transcript) rather than filing-sourced numeric comparable-sales figures; only U.S. Q4 comp of -1.4% is explicitly quantified in the excerpt.
  - A 2025 net restaurant expansion target is mentioned, but it is not a comparable-sales forecast and remains unresolved for comparable sales specifically.

Conclusion:
Comparable sales show a clear transition from strong growth in 2023 to near-stagnation/decline in 2024. Global comparable sales increased 12.6% in Q1 2023, remained strongly positive in Q2 2023 (+11.7% for the quarter), and was still up 8.8% in Q3 2023, before falling to +1.9% in Q1 2024 and turning negative in Q2 2024 (-1.0% for the quarter). Late in 2024, the International Operated Markets segment showed negative quarter comps (e.g., -2.1% in Q3 2024), and U.S. comparable sales declined in Q4 2024 (-1.4%), which management attributed to the E. coli outbreak tied to Quarter Pounders. Overall, the evidence supports that comparable sales volatility in 2024 was driven by both macro/operational deceleration and discrete food-safety/event shocks.

[CORE RESEARCH] MCD: Operating margin

Quarters searched: 2
Filing sections searched: 37

Across 2023, the company guided to 2023 operating margin percent of about 45% in Q1 and Q2, then slightly increased the expectation to about 46% in Q3 (with adjusted operating margin expected to be about 47% when highlighting restructuring-related effects). Reported non-GAAP operating margins in 2023 ranged from 47.1% to 47.5%, clustering in the mid-to-high 40s. In 2024, guidance shifted to a mid-to-high 40% range (Q1 and Q2 and repeated in Q3), while non-GAAP operating margin moved down in Q1 (44.9%) and was higher again in Q2 (44.9% vs 46.0% comparative in the excerpt is presented as a two-period table) and referenced as nearly 47% year-to-date adjusted operating margin in Q3. For 2025, in Q4 2024 the company targeted full-year operating margin percent in the mid-to-high 40% range and an adjusted operating margin above 46.3% (relative to 2024). The provided evidence includes 2024 full-year adjusted operating margin of just over 46% in Q4 commentary, but no 2025 actual results are shown in the excerpt set.

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance/actual pairs were provided as a count for this topic that would allow a Met/Exceeded vs Missed credibility assessment, and the evidence does not include resolved comparisons of each specific guidance figure to a later reported actual for the same period. Therefore, forecasting credibility cannot be rated based on the required guidance-versus-actual pair methodology.

Trends:
  - 2023 non-GAAP operating margin stayed in the mid-to-high 40% range (~47%+) despite slightly changing guidance
  - 2024 guidance remained mid-to-high 40% even as quarterly non-GAAP operating margin fluctuated (e.g., 44.9% in Q1)
  - Stated durability narrative strengthened by Q3/Q4 using adjusted operating margin and restaurant margin dollars

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Pressure on consumer spending environment
  - Cost pressures partially offset top-line growth (explicitly referenced for company-operated margin in Q4)
  - Use of adjusted/non-GAAP operating margin that excludes current/prior year charges and gains (recurring reconciliation)

Key concerns:
  - Forecasting assessment is constrained: the excerpts provide operating margin guidance for 2023/2024/2025 but do not present complete guidance-versus-later-actual pairs needed for a credibility rating under the specified rules.
  - The company repeatedly frames operating margin using exclusions (current/prior year charges and gains), making comparisons across periods dependent on which items were excluded each time.
  - There is limited visibility in the provided excerpts into GAAP operating margin behavior versus non-GAAP/adjusted measures.

Conclusion:
Operating margin, as presented via non-GAAP/adjusted measures, appears to have been resilient and generally stable in the mid-to-high 40% range across 2023 and into 2024. In 2023, reported non-GAAP operating margins were 47.1% (Q2 2023) and 47.5% (Q3 2023) as shown in the filing excerpt set, while guidance was about 45% in Q1/Q2 and about 46% in Q3. In 2024, guidance stayed in the mid-to-high 40% range, and the Q3 2024 transcript indicates year-to-date adjusted operating margin of nearly 47%. For 2025, management targeted mid-to-high 40% operating margin and an adjusted operating margin above 46.3% (relative to 2024), while Q4 commentary references 2024 adjusted operating margin as just over 46%—supporting the view that margin performance is holding up despite cost pressure, though 2025 actuals are not included in the provided evidence.
