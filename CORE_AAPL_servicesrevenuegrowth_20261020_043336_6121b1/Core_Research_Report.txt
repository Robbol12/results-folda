[CORE RESEARCH] AAPL: services revenue growth

Quarters searched: 1
Filing sections searched: 82

What the sources show:
Across Q1 through Q4 2025, services net sales grew year-over-year, consistently attributed to higher net sales from advertising, the App Store and cloud services. The gross margin of services also increased year-over-year each period, with management attributing improvements largely to higher services net sales and/or a different services mix. Services gross margin percentage increased as well throughout 2025, though the drivers evolved from mix effects (Q1) to mix plus partially offsetting factors including higher services costs and weaker foreign currencies (Q2), and later largely mix with partial offset from higher costs (Q3, Q4). In Q3, management further characterized services performance as “very strong” and cited an “all-time record” services revenue of $27.4 billion, up 13%. Overall, the filings show services growth remained broad-based across the year and was accompanied by mix-driven margin expansion despite cost and foreign-currency headwinds in parts of 2025.

Watch points:
  - While the direction of margin improvements is consistently positive, multiple periods note partial offsets from higher services costs, suggesting profitability is sensitive to cost trends.
  - Q2 explicitly references weakness in foreign currencies relative to the U.S. dollar as a partially offsetting factor, indicating currency volatility could affect reported services gross margin percentage.
  - Reliance on advertising, App Store, and cloud services as primary growth sources
  - Services mix changes as a key driver of margin improvements
  - Higher services costs can partially offset margin gains
  - Foreign currency weakness relative to the U.S. dollar can pressure gross margin percentage
