Throughout 2022, Tesla management consistently communicated that the Cybertruck was in development with production readiness efforts ongoing, targeting volume production in mid-2023. The quarterly filings uniformly categorized Cybertruck as 'In development' or 'Tooling' with no production start reported. Management highlighted design progress, manufacturing preparations at Giga Texas, and integration of new hardware such as FSD Hardware 4. Despite repeated affirmations of production timing expectations, no specific forward guidance with quantifiable targets was provided, and the filings cautioned about potential delays similar to other new product launches.

Tesla management has maintained a steady narrative that Cybertruck production would commence in mid-2023, supported by ongoing development and manufacturing preparations. However, the absence of any forward guidance with quantifiable targets or actual production data in filings limits the ability to verify these claims.
