[CORE RESEARCH] TSLA: cybertruck

Quarters searched: 4
Filing sections searched: 97

Throughout 2022, Tesla management consistently communicated that the Cybertruck was in development with production readiness efforts ongoing, targeting volume production in mid-2023. The quarterly filings uniformly categorized Cybertruck as 'In development' or 'Tooling' with no production start reported. Management highlighted design progress, manufacturing preparations at Giga Texas, and integration of new hardware such as FSD Hardware 4. Despite repeated affirmations of production timing expectations, no specific forward guidance with quantifiable targets was provided, and the filings cautioned about potential delays similar to other new product launches.

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance versus actual pairs were provided for the Cybertruck topic, preventing any assessment of forecasting accuracy or credibility. Management statements were qualitative and timing expectations were reiterated without numeric targets or subsequent actual results to compare. Therefore, forecasting credibility cannot be rated based on the available evidence.

Trends:
  - Consistent target of mid-2023 production
  - Ongoing development and tooling progress
  - Emphasis on manufacturing readiness at Giga Texas
  - Integration of advanced hardware (FSD Hardware 4)
  - Repeated caution about potential delays

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Production ramp delays common to new products
  - Uncertainty in launch timing
  - Complexity of new vehicle platform design
  - Reliance on manufacturing capacity expansion

Key concerns:
  - No concrete production or delivery figures reported in filings despite repeated production timing guidance
  - Consistent filing language indicating 'In development' or 'Tooling' status without progress to active production
  - Potential risk of delays as highlighted in multiple filings alongside other new products

Conclusion:
Tesla management has maintained a steady narrative that Cybertruck production would commence in mid-2023, supported by ongoing development and manufacturing preparations. However, the absence of any forward guidance with quantifiable targets or actual production data in filings limits the ability to verify these claims. The filings consistently classify Cybertruck as still in development or tooling stages, underscoring that volume production had not yet begun by the end of 2022. While management expresses confidence in the timeline, the repeated caution about delays and lack of concrete data suggest that investors should remain cautious about the timing and impact of Cybertruck production ramp.
