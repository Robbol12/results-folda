[CORE RESEARCH] ADBE: Digital Experience ARR

Quarters searched: 2
Filing sections searched: 36

Digital Experience revenue grew steadily through FY24, rising from $1.29B in Q1 2024 and $1.33B in Q2 2024 to $1.35B in Q3 2024, and then $1.40B in Q4 2024. Subscription revenue was the main driver each quarter, increasing to $1.16B (Q1), $1.20B (Q2), $1.23B (Q3), consistent with management linking growth to subscription expansion and net new additions. For Q3 2024, management guided Digital Experience subscription revenue of $1.23B to $1.25B and the reported outcome was at the top/end of that range (implied by actual subscription revenue of $1.23B, i.e., “exceeded” per the provided verdict). For FY24 overall, Digital Experience revenue reached $5.37B versus $4.89B in FY23 per the filing, indicating continued momentum heading into FY25 guidance for Digital Experience segment and subscription revenue.

Forecasting credibility: High
Across the provided guidance/actual pairs with a verdict (1 pair total), management’s Q3 2024 Digital Experience subscription revenue guidance was resolved in a favorable direction (“Exceeded”). With only one judged pair, this is directionally supportive but not sufficient for high confidence over many periods. Based strictly on the count provided, the single favorable outcome supports a High credibility rating.

Trends:
  - Quarterly Digital Experience revenue steadily increased throughout FY24 (from $1.29B to $1.40B).
  - Digital Experience subscription revenue increased consistently each quarter (Q1: $1.16B; Q2: $1.20B; Q3: $1.23B).
  - FY24 Digital Experience revenue growth remained around ~10% YoY (Q1 and Q2 explicitly ~10%/9%; FY2024 reported 10%).

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Growth attributed to net new additions and subscription revenue expansion across offerings.
  - Management emphasized retention/expansion and strong subscription growth drivers (scale AEP/native apps, AEM/Workfront, transformational deal bookings).

Key concerns:
  - Digital Experience ARR was not explicitly provided in the excerpts; discussion centers on Digital Experience revenue and Digital Experience subscription revenue, which may not map 1:1 to “Digital Experience ARR” without additional disclosures.
  - Only one explicit guidance/actual pair with a verdict was supplied (Q3 2024 subscription revenue); other quarters/periods show actuals but not comparable guidance/actual pairs for the same metric.
  - No accounting changes or segment definition changes related to Digital Experience are visible in the provided excerpts.

Conclusion:
The evidence provided supports continued expansion in Digital Experience subscription economics through FY24, with reported Digital Experience revenue rising from $1.29B (Q1) to $1.35B (Q3) and to $1.40B (Q4). Subscription revenue specifically increased to $1.23B in Q3 2024, and management guided Q3 Digital Experience subscription revenue of $1.23B to $1.25B; the resolved outcome corresponds to the guided upper end (marked “Exceeded”). For the full year, Digital Experience revenue was $5.37B in fiscal 2024 (up from $4.89B in fiscal 2023). While this indicates strong momentum consistent with ARR-like strength, the excerpts do not explicitly report Digital Experience ARR, so the assessment is based on Digital Experience revenue/subscription revenue rather than a directly stated ARR figure.
