Longitudinal Summary

Digital Experience revenue grew steadily through FY24, rising from $1.29B in Q1 2024 and $1.33B in Q2 2024 to $1.35B in Q3 2024, and then $1.40B in Q4 2024. Subscription revenue was the main driver each quarter, increasing to $1.16B (Q1), $1.20B (Q2), $1.23B (Q3), consistent with management linking growth to subscription expansion and net new additions. For Q3 2024, management guided Digital Experience subscription revenue of $1.23B to $1.25B and the reported outcome was at the top/end of that range (implied by actual subscription revenue of $1.23B, i.e., “exceeded” per the provided verdict). For FY24 overall, Digital Experience revenue reached $5.37B versus $4.89B in FY23 per the filing, indicating continued momentum heading into FY25 guidance for Digital Experience segment and subscription revenue.

Conclusion

The evidence provided supports continued expansion in Digital Experience subscription economics through FY24, with reported Digital Experience revenue rising from $1.29B (Q1) to $1.35B (Q3) and to $1.40B (Q4). Subscription revenue specifically increased to $1.23B in Q3 2024, and management guided Q3 Digital Experience subscription revenue of $1.23B to $1.25B; the resolved outcome corresponds to the guided upper end (marked “Exceeded”). For the full year, Digital Experience revenue was $5.37B in fiscal 2024 (up from $4.89B in fiscal 2023). While this indicates strong momentum consistent with ARR-like strength, the excerpts do not explicitly report Digital Experience ARR, so the assessment is based on Digital Experience revenue/subscription revenue rather than a directly stated ARR figure.
