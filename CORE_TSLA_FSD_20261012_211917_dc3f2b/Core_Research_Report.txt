Quarters searched: 4

What the sources show:
Across Q1–Q2 2021, management framed FSD as a potential source of recurring revenue via subscription, emphasizing the transition risk from customers buying FSD upfront/via leases to customers moving to subscription. In Q2, Tesla launched Full Self-Driving subscription (“last month”) and expected momentum to build over time, while repeatedly indicating that it was still early and that widely availability of FSD (at least beta) was necessary for subscription to take off. In Q3, management described FSD as still in beta and “invitation only” for purposes of deferred revenue recognition, while expanding the FSD City Streets beta to more drivers in October using a “Safety Score.” By Q4, management highlighted strong iteration velocity (seven FSD beta updates in the quarter) and scale growth to nearly 60,000 FSD beta vehicles in the US, while also explicitly linking product/software economics to longer-term profitability from FSD software. In parallel, SEC disclosures consistently characterize deferred revenue as tied to access to features including FSD and indicate that revenue recognition for FSD features occurs when functionality is delivered.

Watch points:
  - Subscription performance is repeatedly described as not meaningful/too early to interpret, implying potential uncertainty in near-term subscription outcomes (Q2).
  - FSD capability characterization in Q3 is described externally by an analyst as “level 2 plus” needing vigilance, while management links rollout to safety metrics and deferred revenue recognition to beta/invitation-only status—highlighting potential mismatch between perceived capability and accounting treatment (Q3).
  - SEC deferred revenue disclosures include FSD as part of a bundle (with supercharger/internet/over-the-air updates), which can make it harder to isolate FSD-specific financial impact without further disaggregation (multiple quarters).
  - Dependency on widely available FSD beta for subscription to “take off”
  - Transition risk from upfront/lease purchases to subscription model
  - Regulatory/safety scrutiny and the need for careful representation of FSD capabilities
  - FSD still treated as beta/incomplete for certain accounting recognition purposes
