Longitudinal Summary

Across 2023-2024, the filings consistently describe overall revenue growth drivers in a more indirect way, emphasizing production and asset-growth initiatives (notably the PDC Energy acquisition) rather than providing explicit revenue guidance. While the excerpts provided focus primarily on oil-equivalent production and capital allocation language, they show a clear operating expansion from PDC and Permian Basin growth, which is the main underlying mechanism likely associated with revenue growth. In 2024, production growth continues year-over-year (full-year of legacy PDC and Permian growth), supporting the continuation of the revenue-growth backdrop. By early 2025, the Future Growth Project (FGP) begins production, further extending the operational growth narrative that would typically translate into revenue growth over time.

Key Concerns

  • No accounting or reporting changes affecting revenue are evidenced in the excerpts provided.

Conclusion

Based on the provided filing excerpts, the evidence supports continued operating growth (oil-equivalent production increases) driven mainly by PDC Energy and Permian Basin performance, which typically correlates with revenue growth. For example, production increases are described as up ~4% in 2023 (mainly due to PDC and Permian growth) and up ~7% in 2024 (mainly due to full-year legacy PDC and Permian growth). However, the excerpts do not provide explicit revenue growth guidance or actual revenue outcomes, so revenue growth cannot be directly confirmed from the supplied evidence—only the underlying operational factors that would likely support it.
