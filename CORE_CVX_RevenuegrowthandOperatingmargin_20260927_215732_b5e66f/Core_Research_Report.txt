For example, production increases are described as up ~4% in 2023 (mainly due to PDC and Permian growth) and up ~7% in 2024 (mainly due to full-year legacy PDC and Permian growth). However, the excerpts do not provide explicit revenue growth guidance or actual revenue outcomes, so revenue growth cannot be directly confirmed from the supplied evidence—only the underlying operational factors that would likely support it.

Quarters searched: 2

Across the provided excerpts, management discussed operating-margins primarily in descriptive, scenario-based terms rather than providing specific margin targets or numeric ranges. In Q1 2024, management highlighted that margins can shift between different refinery margin “buckets” (e.g., crush vs. upgrading vs. marketing/refining). In Q2 2024, management linked margin performance to timing of turnaround activity occurring during a more favorable portion of the quarter, with an accompanying transcript note that such activity benefited from “the more attractive margin portion.” No additional periods include explicit operating margin results or forward guidance figures, and Q2 2024 is the only period where a contemporaneous directional explanation of margin movement is paired with an “actual” statement (without a numeric margin disclosed here).

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance/actual pairs were provided for operating margin in the evidence set, so forecasting accuracy cannot be assessed. Management commentary in Q1 2024 and Q2 2024 is qualitative (margin regime shifts and timing effects) and does not include a stated forward margin figure to compare against a later reported operating margin number.

Trends:
  - Qualitative emphasis on margin regime shifts
  - Margin performance attributed to operational timing (turnaround occurring during favorable margin window)

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Margin volatility driven by shifting operating regimes (crush/upgrading/refining/marketing buckets)
  - Turnaround and other operational activities can materially affect realized margins depending on timing

Key concerns:
  - No explicit operating margin guidance or numeric operating margin actuals are provided in the excerpts, limiting the ability to evaluate margin level, direction, or sustainability.
  - Because only qualitative explanations are shown, there is no evidence here to reconcile management’s described drivers with a quantified operating margin outcome.

Conclusion:
Based on the provided evidence, operating margin discussion is framed as a function of changing margin regimes and the timing of turnaround/turnover activities, rather than as a metric managed against a forecast. In Q1 2024, management indicated margins can move among different margin types (“crush,” “upgrading,” “marketing/refining”). In Q2 2024, management attributed performance to turnaround activity occurring during “the more attractive margin portion of the quarter.” However, without guidance-vs-actual pairs or filing-sourced numeric operating margin figures in the provided excerpts, the operating margin trajectory and management forecasting credibility cannot be quantified from this dataset.
