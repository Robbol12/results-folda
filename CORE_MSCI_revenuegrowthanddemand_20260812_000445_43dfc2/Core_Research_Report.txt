[CORE RESEARCH] MSCI: revenue growth

Quarters searched: 5
Filing sections searched: 80

Over the observed periods from Q1 2023 through Q4 2024, the company demonstrated consistent revenue growth, with total operating revenues increasing steadily each quarter and year. Recurring subscription revenues, particularly from Index products, ESG and Climate products, Analytics, and Private Assets, were key drivers of this growth. Asset-based fees showed some variability but generally trended upward, especially in 2024. Management consistently highlighted strong organic revenue growth and profitability, with reported figures from filings confirming these trends. The company also noted significant growth in non-recurring revenues in 2023, which partially offset declines in some asset-based fee segments. By 2024, revenue growth remained robust, supported by acquisitions such as Burgiss and strong performance across all product lines.

Forecasting credibility: High
Management's guidance on revenue growth was credible, as evidenced by the single guidance/actual pair available for Q4 2024, where the company met its organic revenue growth target of almost 10%. Throughout the periods, management's forward-looking statements about maintaining high levels of revenue growth were supported by actual reported increases in total operating revenues and recurring subscription revenues. The consistency between management's commentary and filing-reported results across multiple quarters further supports a high rating for forecasting credibility.

Trends:
  - steady total revenue growth
  - strong recurring subscription revenue growth
  - growth in Index, ESG & Climate, Analytics, and Private Assets products
  - increasing asset-based fee revenues in 2024
  - non-recurring revenue growth in 2023

Recurring themes/risks:
  - impact of foreign currency fluctuations
  - variability in asset-based fee revenues
  - importance of recurring subscription revenue
  - growth driven by acquisitions (e.g., Burgiss)
  - management judgment in revenue growth assumptions

Key concerns:
  - No significant inconsistencies or accounting changes were noted across the filings; however, the company’s reliance on management judgment for key assumptions in valuation and revenue growth estimates introduces some subjectivity.
  - Non-recurring revenues showed large increases in 2023, which may not be sustainable long-term and could affect comparability.
  - Asset-based fee revenues experienced some declines in 2023 before rebounding in 2024, indicating some volatility in this revenue stream.

Conclusion:
The company exhibited consistent and solid revenue growth throughout the periods analyzed, driven primarily by recurring subscription revenues across multiple product lines and supported by asset-based fees and non-recurring revenues. Management's guidance on revenue growth was met or closely aligned with actual results, reinforcing their credibility. Organic revenue growth remained strong, near or above 10% annually, with particular strength in Private Assets and ESG & Climate products. While some variability in asset-based fees and non-recurring revenues exists, the overall trajectory is positive, indicating a healthy and growing revenue base.

Over the periods from Q4 2023 through Q4 2024, demand for MSCI's products has been characterized by a strong base of subscription and recurring revenue, with about 96% of revenue coming from these sources. Management highlighted ongoing demand in emerging markets despite nascent adoption, and noted challenges in sectors like commercial real estate. However, demand for products uncorrelated with transaction volumes, such as portfolio services and climate tools, showed growth, particularly in Europe. Regulatory and disclosure requirements have been key drivers of demand for sustainability and climate-related solutions, although industry fee pressures and political uncertainties pose risks to future demand.

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance versus actual pairs on demand were provided in the evidence, so it is not possible to assess management's forecasting credibility on this topic. Without forward guidance and corresponding actual results, no pattern of met, exceeded, or missed expectations can be established. Therefore, the forecasting credibility rating must remain null.

Trends:
  - High recurring revenue base
  - Emerging market demand nascent but growing
  - Growth in sustainability and climate-related product demand
  - Challenges in commercial real estate sector
  - Regulatory-driven demand in Europe

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Regulatory and political uncertainty
  - Industry fee pressure and vendor consolidation
  - Client self-sufficiency potentially reducing demand
