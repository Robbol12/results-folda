[CORE RESEARCH] LMT: demand

Quarters searched: 2
Filing sections searched: 63

Throughout 2023, management consistently reported strong and strengthening international demand for key products such as the F-35 aircraft, missile defense systems like PAC-3 and THAAD, and other advanced technologies. The company highlighted commitments and purchase intents from countries including Canada and Singapore, and noted increased demand driven by U.S. government security assistance to Ukraine. Backlog reached a record $161 billion by Q4 2023, reflecting robust demand across multiple business segments. Management also emphasized ongoing efforts to evaluate and increase production capacity to meet anticipated demand.

Forecasting credibility: None
No explicit forward guidance paired with actual results was provided for demand, preventing an assessment of forecasting credibility. Management commentary consistently described demand as strong or strengthening, but without quantifiable guidance-actual pairs, no credibility rating can be assigned. The assessment is therefore based solely on qualitative statements rather than measurable forecasting performance.

Trends:
  - Strong international demand
  - Increasing backlog
  - Capacity evaluation and expansion
  - Growing demand from government security assistance
  - Broad portfolio demand growth

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Dependence on government budgets and priorities
  - Supply chain and production capacity constraints
  - Geopolitical factors influencing demand (e.g., Ukraine conflict)
  - International customer commitments and options

Key concerns:
  - No quantitative forward guidance on demand was provided, limiting transparency on management's forecasting accuracy.
  - Potential risks from unpredictable government budget impacts due to COVID-19 and shifting priorities were noted but not quantified.
  - No accounting or reporting inconsistencies were evident in the demand disclosures.

Conclusion:
Demand for the company's products remained robust and even strengthened throughout 2023, supported by international customer commitments and increased government-driven orders. The record backlog and ongoing capacity evaluations indicate strong confidence in sustained demand. However, the absence of explicit forward guidance paired with actual results on demand metrics means that while qualitative evidence points to healthy demand, the precision of management's demand forecasting cannot be verified. Overall, demand appears solid and growing, but transparency on forecasting remains limited.
