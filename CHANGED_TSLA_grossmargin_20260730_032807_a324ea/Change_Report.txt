[WHAT CHANGED SINCE LAST QUARTER] TSLA: gross margin

Quarters compared: TSLA_Q3_2025, TSLA_Q4_2025
Change detected: True

Management reported a meaningful improvement in automotive gross margins from Q3 to Q4 2025, with margins excluding credits rising from 15.4% to 17.9%. Despite a 16% decline in deliveries, gross profit remained flat due to a favorable regional mix. Additionally, Q4 marked a record total gross margin of over 20.1%, the highest in over two years, achieved despite tariff impacts and increased operating expenses. The transition to a subscription model for FSD was also highlighted as a new factor influencing margins going forward.
