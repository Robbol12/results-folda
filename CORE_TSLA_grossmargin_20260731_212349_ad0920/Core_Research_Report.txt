[CORE RESEARCH] TSLA: gross margin

Quarters searched: 4
Filing sections searched: 97

Over the course of 2024, the company's gross margin for total automotive showed a general decline compared to the prior year periods, with Q1 and Q2 reflecting decreases from 21.1% and 19.2% to 18.5% respectively. However, in Q3 2024, there was a notable increase in the quarterly automotive gross margin from 18.7% to 20.1%, though the nine-month cumulative margin still declined year-over-year. The total automotive & services and other segment margins generally followed a similar downward trend, with some quarterly fluctuations. The energy generation and storage segment experienced a significant gross margin improvement in Q1 2024. Management acknowledged the challenges in maintaining margins in the service and other business, expecting them to remain lower than the rest of the business. The full year 2024 gross margin for total automotive decreased from 19.4% to 18.4%, consistent with management's guidance.

Forecasting credibility: High
There is one explicit guidance/actual pair for the full year 2024 where management's expectation of lower automotive gross margin was met exactly, with reported gross margin decreasing from 19.4% to 18.4%. This alignment between guidance and actual results supports a high credibility rating. Although no other explicit guidance/actual pairs were provided, the consistency of reported margins and management commentary across quarters further reinforces the reliability of their forecasts.

Trends:
  - Declining automotive gross margin year-over-year in early 2024
  - Improvement in automotive gross margin in Q3 2024 quarter
  - Energy generation and storage gross margin increased sharply in Q1 2024
  - Service and other segment margins lower than automotive margins
  - Full year automotive gross margin declined modestly

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Lower average selling price impacting automotive gross margin
  - Cybertruck production ramp affecting margins
  - Regulatory credits and FSD revenue partially offsetting margin pressure
  - Service and other business margins remain challenging
  - Volatility in automotive gross margin quarter-to-quarter

Key concerns:
  - No explicit guidance/actual pairs other than full year 2024 limit detailed assessment of forecasting accuracy for interim periods.
  - Sequential decline in service and other business margins despite year-over-year improvement suggests operational challenges.
  - Gross margin fluctuations may reflect product mix changes and ramp-up costs, requiring close monitoring.

Conclusion:
The company's gross margin for total automotive experienced a general decline throughout 2024 compared to prior year periods, driven by lower average selling prices and the Cybertruck ramp, partially offset by regulatory credits and FSD revenue. The energy generation and storage segment showed a strong margin improvement early in the year. Despite some quarterly improvements, the full year 2024 automotive gross margin decreased from 19.4% to 18.4%, consistent with management's guidance. Service and other segment margins remain lower and more volatile. Overall, the gross margin trends reflect ongoing pressures in automotive pricing and production costs, balanced by strategic revenue offsets.
