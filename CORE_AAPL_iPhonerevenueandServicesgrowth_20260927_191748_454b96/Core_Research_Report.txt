Quarters searched: 1
Filing sections searched: 166

Across 2023, iPhone net sales were mixed quarter to quarter: Q2 2023 iPhone net sales were $51.334B vs $50.570B (+2%), while Q3 2023 iPhone net sales were $39.669B vs $40.665B (-2%). By Q4 2023, iPhone net sales were $50.489B vs $47.508B (+7%), contributing to a down year-over-year comparison discussed in the 2023 filing narrative (iPhone net sales decreased 2% or $4.9B during 2023 vs 2022). In 1Q 2024, iPhone net sales rebounded to $69.702B vs $65.775B (+6%) driven primarily by higher Pro model sales, and the filing later characterized first-six-month 2024 iPhone net sales as relatively flat. In the latest quarter provided (Q4 2024), management reported iPhone revenue of $46.2B, up 6% year over year, and the filing narrative stated iPhone net sales were relatively flat during 2024 compared to 2023, despite regional divergence (e.g., Greater China lower, Japan higher).

Conclusion:
iPhone revenue/net sales were positive in several quarters but not consistently: Q2 2023 iPhone net sales were $51.334B (+2%), Q3 2023 were $39.669B (-2%), and Q4 2023 iPhone net sales were $50.489B (+7%). In early 2024, iPhone strength returned in Q1 2024 with $69.702B (+6%) attributed in the filing to higher Pro model sales; however, the filing also described iPhone net sales as relatively flat during the first six months of 2024. Across 2023, Services net sales generally rose year over year, with management attributing growth primarily to higher cloud services, music, advertising, and the App Store. Services gross margin increased in multiple periods even as Services gross margin percentage sometimes declined due to foreign currency weakness and higher Services costs, with later periods citing mix improvements. In 2024, filing disclosures describe Services growing (e.g., Services net sales up 9% and $7.1 billion during 2023 vs. 2022; and in Q4 2024, Services revenue reached $25 billion, up 12% year over year). In Q4 2024, management also guided that services revenue would grow double digits at a rate similar to fiscal 2024; Q1 2025 filings then continued to show Services net sales growth and improving Services gross margin/percentage driven by higher net sales and mix. Overall, the narrative is consistent: Services growth remained supported by advertising/App Store/cloud, while margin dynamics reflected mix and cost/FX pressures.

  - By late 2024, Services reached an all-time revenue record with continued double-digit growth

Conclusion:
