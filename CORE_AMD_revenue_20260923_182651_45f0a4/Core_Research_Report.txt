Across FY2024, revenue recovered from weaker segments (notably gaming and embedded) while data center and client drove most of the upside. In Q1, net revenue was $5.5B, up 2% y/y, aligning with management’s broader narrative of segment mix benefits. In Q2, management guided revenue to ~$6.7B ±$300M, but actual revenue was $5.8B (below the guidance range), while segment commentary continued to emphasize improving order patterns. In Q3, management again referenced far lower revenue ("perhaps $2 billion"), yet reported $6.8B net revenue, indicating that the specific implied full-year framing missed while quarterly results were much stronger than that remark. In Q4, revenue landed at $7.7B, within the stated $7.1B ±$300M range (forecast resolution shown only for the quarterly figure).

Forecasting credibility: Low
From the provided guidance/actual pairs with verdicts, management missed key revenue targets at least twice (Q2 full-quarter revenue level and Q3 “$2 billion” reference) and only met once (Q4 quarterly revenue within ~$7.1B ±$300M). This miss-heavy pattern reduces confidence in management’s revenue forecasting accuracy for the year/quarterly levels, even though the company’s underlying trend (data center/client strength) was directionally consistent.

Trends:
  - Data center and client drove growth while gaming/embedded acted as offsets
  - Seasonality/second-half strength reiterated across the year
  - Quarterly net revenue swung materially around management’s guidance

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Segment mix volatility (gaming/embedded declines offsetting data center/client gains)
  - Order-pattern uncertainty and timing impacts on revenue
  - Reliance on data center GPU ramp assumptions for year-level expectations

Key concerns:
  - Guidance-to-actual mismatch for Q2 revenue level: guided approximately $6.7B ±$300M versus actual $5.8B (below the guided range).
  - Q3 included a much lower revenue expectation reference (“perhaps $2 billion” for 2024) while the quarterly reported revenue was $6.8B; this suggests risk of confusion/misaligned framing for full-year implications.
  - Year-level GPU revenue guidance was revised upward multiple times and still marked as missed in the provided pair set, implying uncertainty in ramp timing.

Conclusion:
Revenue in FY2024 showed a strong rebound driven by data center and client strength, but management’s specific revenue forecasts were less reliable. Reported net revenue was $5.5B in Q1 (up 2% y/y) and $5.8B in Q2 (up 9% y/y) against Q2 guidance of ~$6.7B ±$300M (missed the implied range). Q3 net revenue was $6.8B (up 18% y/y per filing excerpt), despite management’s “perhaps $2 billion” reference for 2024 being marked missed in the guidance/actual set. Q4 revenue was $7.7B (up 24% y/y), and the provided pair set indicates the $7.1B ±$300M guidance was met, suggesting better forecasting precision later in the year despite earlier misses.
