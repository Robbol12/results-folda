Throughout 2020, management discussed margin primarily in the context of operational impacts and segment performance, noting that margin was a relatively small component of overall results and influenced by factors such as hedge positions and acquisitions. The filings consistently described margin requirements as defined by exchange rules and subject to additional requests by clearing agents based on market conditions. There was no explicit forward guidance on margin provided by management in any quarter.
