[CORE RESEARCH] AMZN: revenue growth

Quarters searched: 3
Filing sections searched: 69

Across Q1–Q3 2024, management characterized revenue growth as accelerating through the first three quarters (e.g., 17.2% in Q1 accelerating to 18.8% in Q2, with further commentary on advertising growth in Q3). Guidance for subsequent quarters generally aligned with realized growth rates: Q2 guidance (7%–11% growth) was exceeded by reported consolidated growth of 11% in Q2, and Q3 guidance (8%–11% growth) was met with reported consolidated growth of 11% in Q3. However, for Q4 2024 guidance (7%–11% growth), the reported consolidated growth was 11%, which is above the midpoint but still marked as missed per the provided guidance/actual verdict. By full-year 2024 (as disclosed in the filing excerpt), sales increased 11% year over year, indicating sustained growth despite quarter-to-quarter guidance noise.

Forecasting credibility: Medium
Out of 3 guidance/actual pairs with verdicts provided, management forecasts were Met/Exceeded for 2 and Missed for 1. That pattern is directionally credible but not consistently strong given the single miss. Therefore, overall forecasting credibility for revenue growth is assessed as Medium based strictly on the Met/Exceeded vs Missed count.

Trends:
  - Revenue growth remained in the low-to-mid teens year over year early in the period (as described by management for Q1/Q2).
  - Quarterly reported consolidated growth stayed around ~11% for Q2 and Q3 (from filing growth-rate tables).
  - Full-year 2024 revenue growth remained positive at 11% year over year (from filing excerpt).

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Foreign exchange impacts on reported net sales growth (explicitly noted for 2024).
  - Managing through lower ASP/trade-down dynamics affecting mix (noted in Q3 commentary).
  - AWS growth rate predictability uncertainty referenced by management.

Key concerns:
  - One guidance pair was marked 'Missed' even though the provided filing table shows consolidated growth of 11% for Q4 2024; this suggests the verdict may rely on a different mapping or threshold than the simplified band shown, or on guidance measured against a specific line item/definition not fully visible in the excerpts.
  - The evidence set mixes qualitative commentary about acceleration (Q1/Q2) with quantitative filing tables for growth rates, so the exact guidance-to-actual comparison basis should be confirmed when interpreting the single miss.

Conclusion:
Revenue growth was sustained and largely consistent with management’s stated targets over Q1–Q3 2024. Guidance for Q2 (7%–11%) was exceeded with reported consolidated growth of 11% for the quarter, and guidance for Q3 (8%–11%) was met with reported consolidated growth of 11%. Although Q4 guidance was marked missed in the provided verdicts, the filing excerpted growth rates show consolidated growth of 11% in Q4, and full-year 2024 sales increased 11% year over year. Overall, the totality of guidance/actual pairs and reported filing figures indicates positive revenue growth through the period.
