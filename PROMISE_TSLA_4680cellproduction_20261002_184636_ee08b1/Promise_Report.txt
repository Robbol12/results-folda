[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: 4680 cell production

VERDICT: Fulfilled (confidence: high)

Management initially guided toward volume 4680 production in 2021/2022 (optimistic “next year” in Q1 2021; volume “towards the end of the third quarter and certainly in the fourth quarter” in Q1 2022). By Q4 2021 they were building 4680 structural packs daily and expecting first 4680 vehicles that quarter, and by Q4 2022 they reported achieving 1,000 cells/week. Through 2023-2024 they continued ramping in Texas and reported >1,000/week (Q1 2024) and “more than 1,400…per week” (Q2 2024), indicating delivery at scale relative to stated ramps and cost-down goals.
