[CORE RESEARCH] AMD: revenue

Quarters searched: 3
Filing sections searched: 44

Across 1H 2024, revenue performance improved materially versus the prior year, with Q1 net revenue at $5.5 billion (+2% YoY) and Q2 net revenue at $5.8 billion (+9% YoY). In Q2, management’s revenue outlook (about $6.7B ± $300M) was not resolved in the provided record, while the company’s Data Center GPU revenue expectation was missed (guided to exceed $4.5B in 2024, later outcome marked Missed). Q3 showed a partial but volatile mix, with reported net revenue $6.8B (+18% YoY), while a prior GPU revenue target of exceeding $5B in 2024 was marked Missed. By Q4, reported total revenue was $7.7B (+24% YoY), and the filing indicates full-year net revenue increased 14% to $25.8B versus $22.7B in 2023, aligning with a stronger second-half seasonality narrative.

Forecasting credibility: Low
From the guidance/actual evidence provided, there were multiple misses in revenue-related forecasting beyond any matched “met/exceeded” outcomes (notably Data Center GPU revenue targets were marked Missed in both Q2 and Q3). Even where some revenue directional guidance appears to have been supported by subsequent actuals (e.g., later Q4 total revenue was $7.7B), the pattern of missed key revenue targets leads to low credibility based on the guidance/actual pair verdicts available in the excerpt.

Trends:
  - YoY revenue growth accelerated into the second half of 2024 (Q2 $5.8B, Q3 $6.8B, Q4 $7.7B)
  - Seasonality expectation that 2H is typically higher remained part of management’s narrative
  - Segment/mix volatility (gaming/embedded weakness offset by data center and client strength)

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Data center/client growth offset by declines in gaming/embedded
  - Material uncertainty in GPU/segment-level revenue targets
  - Product transition and market condition effects on timing/trajectory

Key concerns:
  - Data Center GPU revenue guidance was missed in multiple instances: guided to exceed $4.5B (April) but outcome was marked Missed, and later guided to exceed $5B in 2024 but again marked Missed.
  - Guidance-related consistency for consolidated revenue appears less reliable in the provided verdict set, with several guidance points left Unresolved and key target misses for revenue components.

Conclusion:
Revenue increased substantially across 2024, from $5.5B net revenue in Q1 (+2% YoY) to $5.8B in Q2 (+9% YoY), $6.8B in Q3 (+18% YoY), and a record $7.7B in Q4 (+24% YoY). The company’s filing reports full-year net revenue of $25.8B, up 14% from $22.7B in 2023, indicating the overall revenue uptrend was realized. However, revenue forecasting credibility is constrained by repeated misses on revenue-critical GPU targets (marked Missed for exceeding $4.5B and for exceeding $5B in 2024), suggesting forecast precision was weaker at the component level even as consolidated results improved.
