[CORE RESEARCH] AMZN: revenue growth

Quarters searched: 5
Filing sections searched: 138

Management repeatedly highlighted strengthening year-over-year revenue growth in 2024—accelerating to 17.2% in Q1 (up from 13.2% in Q4 per transcript commentary) and then to 18.8% in Q2 (with additional commentary that sequential growth was influenced by ASP trends). Filing-sourced actuals show positive top-line growth throughout 2024 (e.g., sales +13% in Q1 and +10% in Q2) while also citing foreign exchange as a headwind to net sales. In Q3 2024, management also pointed to advertising revenue growth (18.8% YoY) alongside continued category strength, with foreign exchange again reducing reported net sales. By 2024 full-year, filing-sourced results indicate sales increased 11% and AWS sales increased 19%, supporting the narrative of continued growth concentrated in AWS. In 2025, the evidence provided is more sparse for consolidated revenue growth rates, but at least one transcript statement indicates very strong quarter-over-quarter growth (150% QoQ in Q3 2025) and a transcript characterization of 24% YoY growth (Q4 2025).

Forecasting credibility: None
No guidance/actual pairs for revenue growth were provided in the evidence, so forecasting credibility cannot be assessed using the required Met/Missed methodology. The topic’s discussion appears largely descriptive (commentary on observed growth rates) rather than tied to explicit forward-looking revenue-growth guidance that later resolves against actuals.

Trends:
  - YoY revenue growth narrative accelerated in early 2024 (Q1 and Q2 commentary)
  - Foreign exchange cited as a recurring headwind to reported net sales
  - AWS growth remained a standout contributor (19% for 2024 full-year in filings)
  - 2025 shows indications of strong growth in transcript excerpts, but limited filing-sourced consolidated growth figures provided

Recurring themes/risks:
  - Foreign exchange rate impacts on net sales
  - Sequential revenue dynamics influenced by ASP and product mix
  - Ongoing sensitivity to macro/geopolitical and customer demand conditions (risk-factor language in filings)

Key concerns:
  - The evidence set includes management statements about revenue growth rates (e.g., 17.2% in Q1; 18.8% in Q2) but does not provide explicit forward guidance on revenue growth that can be compared to later actuals, preventing a guidance-vs-actual credibility check.
  - Some growth assertions are from transcripts while filing actuals provided are sometimes more granular (e.g., sales % changes and FX headwinds) rather than matching the exact consolidated 'revenue growth rate' metric management quoted.

Conclusion:
Revenue growth was consistently positive across 2024 in the provided filing-sourced actuals: sales increased 13% in Q1 2024, sales increased 10% in Q2 2024, and sales increased 11% for full-year 2024. FX headwinds were repeatedly disclosed in filings (e.g., Q1 2024: reduced net sales by $164 million; Q3 2024: reduced net sales by $168 million), indicating that underlying demand/growth was strong enough to offset some translation effects. AWS specifically outperformed, with AWS sales increasing 19% in 2024 per filings, aligning with management’s emphasis on AWS growth. For 2025, the provided evidence includes transcript characterizations of very strong growth (150% QoQ in Q3 2025; 24% YoY with a ~$142B annualized run rate in Q4 2025), but there are no filing-sourced, consolidated full-year/quarter revenue growth rates included here to quantify the full-year trend with the same precision as 2024.

Evidence Coverage: 99.2% complete; 358/361 claims complete. This is an evidence inventory, not an objective truth score.
