Over the periods from Q1 2021 through Q4 2021, the company consistently disclosed plans and investments in renewable energy projects, particularly solar photovoltaic and battery energy storage systems (ESS) in Texas and Illinois. The scale of projects evolved from an initial 668 MW solar and 260 MW battery ESS in Texas to additional projects including 300 MW solar and 150 MW battery ESS in Illinois, supported by legislative developments such as Illinois Senate Bill 2408. Management articulated ambitions to grow the renewable and battery storage portfolio to over five gigawatts and generate significant adjusted EBITDA by 2026, though these targets remain unresolved. The filings show steady capital accumulation in construction work-in-process for these projects, reflecting ongoing development efforts.
