[PROMISE TRACKING] TSLA: FSD

VERDICT: Delayed (confidence: high)

Management consistently discussed plans and early pilots for the FSD subscription and beta rollout from Q1 2021 through 
Q4 2022, highlighting gradual progress but no clear indication of full-scale delivery. The subscription was launched in 
Q2 2021 with expectations of slow build, and by Q2 2022, the FSD beta with city streets capability was deployed to over 
100,000 owners, indicating pilot scale. However, throughout the period, statements remained cautious and uncertain about
widespread adoption and revenue recognition, suggesting the promise has been delayed rather than fully fulfilled or 
abandoned.
