[CORE RESEARCH] AAPL: revenue growth

Quarters searched: 1
Filing sections searched: 84

What the sources show:
Across Q1 2024 through Q4 2024, Apple’s reported revenue growth remained modest. In Q1 2024, total net sales increased 2% year over year, driven by higher iPhone and Services sales, partially offset by declines in iPad and Wearables, Home and Accessories. By Q3 2024, the company disclosed operating expense levels as a percentage of total net sales, but no revenue-growth rate was provided in the excerpt; however, the emphasis remained on net sales composition rather than a shift in growth direction. In Q4 2024, management reported products revenue of $70 billion up 4% year over year and reiterated expectations that total company revenue would grow low to mid-single digits, with services revenue expected to grow double digits at a rate similar to fiscal 2024. Additionally, management’s guidance referenced Apple Intelligence rolling out over time and product launches, suggesting an ongoing driver of future growth.

Watch points:
  - Q1 2024 growth is characterized as mixed across categories (iPhone and Services up, iPad and Wearables/Home/Accessories down), implying potential volatility in consolidated results if offsets weaken.
  - Deferred revenue timing percentages shift from Q1 to Q3 2024 (e.g., less-than-a-year share 66% in Q1 vs. 64% in Q3), which could indicate changes in revenue recognition patterns, though the excerpts do not provide causal detail.
  - In Q4 2024, management did not provide iPhone-specific growth color despite an analyst request, limiting transparency into whether iPhone is expected to grow faster than the company’s overall low-to-mid single digit outlook.
  - Dependence on iPhone and Services growth to offset declines in other categories
  - Services growth framed as a key contributor to overall revenue performance
  - International/global business as a majority of net sales

Management commentary:
  - Products revenue was $70 billion, up 4% year over year, driven by growth in iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  - Yes, we've said that we expect total company revenue to grow low to mid-single digits.
  - Revenue was $7.7 billion, up 2% from a year ago.
  - We expect services revenue to grow double digits at a rate similar to what we reported in the fiscal year 2024.
  - We expect our December quarter total company revenue to grow low to mid-single digits year over year.
  - Services revenue reached an all-time record of $25 billion, up 12% year over year.
  - Today, Apple is reporting revenue of $94.9 billion, a September quarter record and up 6% from a year ago.
  - iPhone grew in every geographic segment, marking a new September quarter revenue record for the category, and services set an all-time revenue record, up 12% year over year.
  - And we continue to be excited by the enthusiasm we're seeing in India, where we set an all-time revenue record.
  - iPad revenue was $7 billion, 8% higher year over year.
  - In Wearables, Home, and Accessories, revenue was $9 billion, down 3% from a year ago.
  - As I mentioned earlier, Services achieved an all-time revenue record of $25 billion, up 12% from a year ago and with all-time revenue records across most of our categories.
  - We are very pleased to report a new September quarter revenue record of $94.9 billion, up 6% year over year.
  - We grew in the vast majority of the markets we track and achieved September quarter revenue records in the Americas, Europe, and rest of Asia Pacific.
  - iPhone revenue was $46.2 billion, up 6% year over year and a September quarter record in total and across several markets, including the U.S., the Middle East, Korea, and South Asia.
  - We continue to see high levels of customer satisfaction for the iPhone 15 family with 451 Research recently measuring it at 98% in the U.S. Mac revenue was $7.7 billion, up 2% year over year, driven by the strength in MacBook Air.
  - And in the latest reports from 451 Research, customer satisfaction was 95% in the U.S. iPad generated $7 billion in revenue, up 8% year over year.
  - Also, customer satisfaction was recently measured at 97% in the U.S. Wearables, Home, and Accessories revenue was $9 billion, down 3% year over year.
  - And the latest reports from 451 Research indicated customer satisfaction of 96% for Watch in the U.S. Our Services revenue reached an all-time record of $25 billion, growing 12% year over year.
  - And we also include India, where we set an all-time revenue record during the September quarter.
